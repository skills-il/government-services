# Israel Land Authority (RMI) System Guide

## Israeli Land System
- About 93% of land is owned by the state or quasi-state agencies and administered through RMI
- Leasehold model: by tender, generally 98 years plus a 98-year option; without a tender, up to 49 years plus a 49-year option
- Some lessees can acquire ownership under sub-chapter 5.1 of the council decisions; check the conditions with RMI

## Affordable Housing Program: Dira BeHanacha
The affordable-housing lottery program, historically called "Mechir Lamishtaken"
(מחיר למשתכן), has been restructured as an umbrella program called "Dira
BeHanacha" (דירה בהנחה) with multiple sub-tracks. It is administered by the
Ministry of Construction and Housing (משרד הבינוי והשיכון), not by RMI directly.
The flow is two-step:

1. RMI tenders the land to developers under special conditions (reduced land price in exchange for below-market unit sale prices).
2. The Ministry of Construction and Housing runs eligibility (teudat zakaut), registration, and the random drawing for end buyers at `dira.moch.gov.il`.

Agents helping users "sign up for mechir lamishtaken" should direct them to
the Housing Ministry registration site, not the RMI tender portal.

### Dira BeHanacha sub-tracks (as of 2026)
| Sub-track | Hebrew | Discount cap | Grant | Audience |
|-----------|--------|--------------|-------|----------|
| General Mechir Lamishtaken | מחיר למשתכן כללי | land subsidised to the developer, read the discount off the project page | periphery grant 40,000 or 60,000 NIS by locality | first-time buyers, and upgraders for units left one year after the first lottery |
| Mehir Matara (Target Price) | מחיר מטרה | 25% off the computed apartment price, capped at 550,000 NIS per unit (council 4.7.3) | periphery grant 40,000 NIS | first-time buyers, and upgraders for units left after the first lottery year |
| Mehir Muphchat (Reduced Price) | מחיר מופחת | earlier track, read the price off the project page | periphery grant 40,000 NIS | per the project terms |
| Dira Lehaskir (Rent) | דירה להשכיר | long-term subsidized rental, not for sale | n/a | first priority to active reservists and bereaved widows or widowers through 31.12.2029 |

### Resale lockup
Cannot sell the unit (including a transfer for free or at a reduced price) for **5 years from the completion certificate (teudat gmar; Kol Zchut says Tofes 4, confirm in the signed undertaking) OR 7 years from the lottery date (the sale-agreement date for a unit bought outside a lottery), whichever comes first**. A transfer inside the period needs Housing Ministry approval, and the seller repays the discount, linked to the CPI from possession. The clause does not mention renting; check the signed undertaking.

### Eligibility certificate
- Application via the registration companies (Milgam, Alonim, Maof)
- Cost: 200 NIS new, 50 NIS renewal
- Required before registering for any lottery round
- Since 24.05.2026 lottery entry also requires that IDF data does not define the applicant or a spouse as draft-obligated without a regularised status

### Lottery rounds (verify before quoting)
- Rounds open and close on their own calendar. Lottery 11 ran in May-June 2026 and has closed.
- Reservist priority and the other draw priorities are in `lottery-and-reservist-rules.md`. Maof states that the November 2026 continuation lotteries had no priorities; confirm per round.
- The eligibility-certificate deadline and the list of localities are set per round. Read them off the Housing Ministry round page.
- In the first series of lotteries a registrant may pick projects in at most 3 localities; in continuation series there is no locality limit.
