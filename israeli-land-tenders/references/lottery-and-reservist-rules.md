# Lottery and Reservist Rules (detail)

Source unless noted: Israel Land Council decisions, version of 04.08.2026 (sub-chapters 4.1, 4.2, 4.5, 4.6, 4.7, 4.8). A newer version may exist. The tender booklet and the round page prevail over this summary.

## Dira BeHanacha: existing property (4.7.19, 4.7.20)

Owning or holding rights in an apartment or land, including partial shares, key money, rights under construction or purchase-group rights, can affect eligibility. The rules and thresholds differ by type of right and by whether the applicant is a first-time buyer or an upgrader. Check the specific holding against the current Israel Land Council decision (sub-chapter 4.7, https://apps.land.gov.il/CouncilDecisions/assets/data/96/CouncilDecisions.pdf) or with the Housing Ministry or a registration company before applying.

Other conditions:

- Israeli citizenship
- A family unit may not apply as an individual. An application that does not match personal status voids the eligibility, the win and the purchase
- A couple marrying within 3 months must prove the marriage; if it does not take place, the certificate or the win is cancelled
- Anyone in the household who already bought through an affordable-housing program cannot join again, as a first-time buyer or as an upgrader

## Upgraders (mishaprey diur, 4.7.20)

Open only to a family unit, a widow or widower, or an individual aged 35+. An upgrader who still owns an apartment must sell it no later than 12 months after occupancy approval of the new one. The existing-property check above applies to upgraders too.

## Priorities in the draw (4.7.17)

Applied in this order, and also, with the necessary changes, to draws under earlier affordable-housing tracks in force since 15.07.2015:

1. **Wheelchair-bound applicants**, then an eligible person whose first-degree relative living with them qualifies: one unit per 30 and at least one. If units remain, a parent of a qualifying child under 21 living with them.
2. **Active reservists**: up to 50% of units in national-priority localities at periphery index 1-4, with up to 25% of the draw going first to combat reservists; up to 35% elsewhere, with up to 15% first to combat reservists.
3. **Local residents (bnei makom)**: up to 20%. Must be chasrey dira, resident in the authority for the last 3 years or 4 of the last 10, counted back from the eligibility application date (a recalculation of eligibility resets that date). A mixed couple qualifies only as spouses. In a regional council, for a locality above 5,000 residents, the quota can be limited to residents of that locality. For units added under the significant-deviation regulations the local share can reach 45% of the added units.

Priorities 1 to 3 together may not exceed 73% of the draw; any excess comes off the local-resident share. The rest is drawn among the remaining chasrey dira.

**Lottery 11 exception.** For the first draw after 24.05.2026, section 4.7.21(b) gave active reservists first priority, up to 50% in all localities and up to 25% for combat reservists, conditional on IDF data arriving by that date (with a split-draw fallback otherwise). That rule was for that draw. Later draws follow the table above unless a newer decision changes it.

**Continuation lotteries.** Maof states that the November 2026 continuation lotteries carried no priorities and were open to upgraders. Confirm per round.

**Confrontation line.** Through 31.12.2028 the local-resident share in confrontation-line localities can reach 60% of Mechir Matara units and 60% of self-build plots (9.24). Clause 9.24(יג)(2) of that temporary order says that while in force, 9.24 prevails over any other provision in the Council decisions. Confirm the result for a specific draw with the Ministry.

## Reservist tests: two different definitions

| Benefit | Test |
|---|---|
| Dira BeHanacha priority and Dira Lehaskir priority (4.7.1) | At least 60 days under Order 8 since 07.10.2023. For Lottery 11 the registration companies published the counted window as 07.10.2023 to 30.04.2026, so later days may not count. Confirm the cutoff for the current round |
| RMI land discount (4.2.12) | Any of: (A) six-year active-reservist certificate; (B) at least 6 years on the reserve roster and 80 reserve days since 2000 within a continuous period of up to 6 calendar years; (C) more than 45 days between 07.10.2023 and the declared end of the Iron Swords war |

"Combat reservist" is defined in 4.2.12 for both benefits. Whether a combat reservist with 46 to 59 days gets lottery priority is not clear from the text, so explain that the text leaves this open, and that the personal area and the IDF appeal window are the available ways to check it.

Use of the land discount: routes A and B up to 6 years from discharge, route C up to 6 years from the end of that war service. Kol Zchut still shows 3 years for route A, so a user between years 3 and 6 should get written confirmation from RMI.

Timing: eligibility must exist no later than the payment dates in the deal or 90 days from deal approval, whichever is earlier. File before the first payment. Kol Zchut notes a credit may be possible when confirmations are filed after payment. A combat reservist also files the IDF summary of qualifying service days.

## RMI land discount for reservists (4.2.13 to 4.2.16)

Applies to **low-rise** residential land only. It is not an extra discount on a Dira BeHanacha apartment.

- Lottery or tender exemption: 35% in area A, 20% in area B, 10% elsewhere, capped at 100,000 NIS (150,000 NIS combat), excluding VAT
- Public tender: 15% off the winning price, same caps
- Purchase through an association that won a public tender: 15% refund on the land component, same caps
- Stacking: an active reservist may combine land-price discounts unless RMI management has excluded the combination. Confirm with RMI in writing
- Spouses: if one is a reservist, the discount covers both spouses' share of the one unit. If both are, each gets the full discount with their own cap, which reaches 300,000 NIS only for two combat reservists

Area classification has exceptions. Urban localities at socio-economic index 7-10 get no land discounts under the area rules, and social-periphery localities in groups A1 and A2 get them for saturated construction only. Check the locality on RMI's list for the specific construction type.

## Lease-fee rates (4.2.2, 4.2.13)

For allocations without a tender and self-build by registration and lottery. The reservist columns apply to **low-rise** housing only, once, for one unit. The chasar diur columns apply to self-build:

| Area | Regular | Reservist | Chasar diur (self-build) | Chasar diur + reservist |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 31% | 16% | 26% | 11% |
| B | 51% | 36% | 46% | 31% |

- The reduced rates stop at a land value of 500,000 NIS per unit (900,000 NIS for a self-build chasar diur), excluding development costs and VAT. Full lease fees apply above that. The ceiling does not apply on the confrontation line, where capitalised lease fees are fully exempt
- The chasar diur rates apply only in localities that met RMI's plot-publication quotas (through 31.03.2028). From 05.05.2028 the reduced rates apply only to plots matching the TAMA 35 size
- A chasar diur using the reduced rate generally cannot transfer rights for 7 years from the lease contract and must live in the property for 5 years after construction ends

## RMI self-build registration and lottery (4.6.2)

"Chasar diur" here means holding a Housing Ministry chasar diur eligibility certificate. Obtain it in time and check the booklet for the date it must be held by. Priority order in national-priority areas:

1. Up to 10% of plots to chasrey diur with disabilities, IDF-disabled first
2. All remaining plots to combat reservists who are chasrey diur, family first
3. Then active reservists who are chasrey diur, family first
4. Then other chasrey diur, family first
5. Any plots left go to other registrants

RMI management may cancel these priorities where significant housing for chasrey diur is being marketed in the same locality. Local-resident quotas can apply (4.5.9): up to 50% of plots in rural localities in priority areas and up to 70% in moshavim, higher in Hevel HaTkuma through 31.12.2028. "IDF-disabled" here means 100% or 100% special disability. A plot allocated to someone who is not a chasar diur does not get the chasar diur rate.

**Reservist-only plots (through 31.12.2029).** In localities in the North, Haifa and South districts outside national-priority areas, and in agricultural-locality expansions in the Center and Jerusalem districts, self-build plots can be marketed 100% to IDF-disabled and reservists, with chasrey diur and families first and, among reservists, combat first. In the North, Haifa and South localities, leftover plots can go to a closed reservist tender with a minimum price of at least 30% of land value, chasrey diur first.

## Other benefits

- **IDF-disabled, 100% special disability (4.2.18):** a one-time tender-exempt self-build plot at 31% of land value. Free transfer only after 5 years from signing the lease (development-agreement route) or 10 years (lease without one)
- **Bereaved widows, widowers and parents (4.2.16a):** of security-forces fallen or hostile-action victims. Exempt from purchase and consent fees and from payment on plot attribution. The Dira Lehaskir first priority covers these widows and widowers, not widows generally
- **Reported, confirm with RMI:** combat reservists with at least 60 days of service in 2026 are exempt from the fee for extending self-build deadlines for 2026. RMI's notice printed the refund deadline as "31.6.2027", a date that does not exist, so apply early
