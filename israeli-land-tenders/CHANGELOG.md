# Changelog

## 1.4.0 - 2026-11-23

Rebuilt the lottery and reservist guidance against the Israel Land Council decisions (version of 04.08.2026).

- Added the IDF-status condition for lottery entry, in force since 24.05.2026.
- Stated the Mechir Matara discount (25%, up to 550,000 NIS per unit) and the draw priorities, including the reservist shares.
- Separated the two reservist service tests: at least 60 days for the lottery, more than 45 days for the land discount.
- Replaced the first-time-buyer and upgrader ownership detail with a pointer to the Council decision. Corrected the widow age rule, the disability routes, the resale lockup and the reservist discount conditions.
- Added certificate timing, financing limits, win cancellation, the self-build lottery order, the bid guarantee formula and the minimum-price rise when fewer than 3 qualifying bids arrive.
- Corrected the RMI call-centre number to *5575.
- Removed the land-value formula. The skill lists the factors to review and does not state a value or a bid amount.
- Marked "Mechir Larocheish" as a legacy term.
