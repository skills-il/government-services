# Israeli Government Bodies Guide

A comprehensive reference for identifying government bodies by their letterhead, reference number patterns, and common document types. Use this when you receive a document and need to figure out who sent it and what they want.

## Tax Authority (רשות המסים בישראל)

**Also known as:** Reshut HaMisim, Israel Tax Authority, Income Tax Authority (מס הכנסה), VAT Authority (מע"מ), Customs (מכס)

**Letterhead identifiers:**
- "רשות המסים בישראל" with the Finance Ministry logo
- May specify division: "אגף מס הכנסה", "אגף המכס ומע"מ"
- Regional office name (e.g., "פקיד שומה תל אביב 1")

**Reference number patterns:**
- Income tax file: 9-digit number (e.g., "תיק 123456789")
- VAT file: "מע"מ" + dealer number
- Assessment number: "שומה מס'" + sequential number

**Common document types:**

| Document | Hebrew Name | What It Means | Urgency |
|----------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| Tax assessment | שומה | Their calculation of what you owe | HIGH - 30 days to object |
| Best judgment assessment | שומה לפי מיטב השפיטה | They calculated without your input (you did not file or they reject your filing) | HIGH - 30 days to object |
| Agreed assessment | שומה בהסכמה | You and the assessor agreed on the amount | LOW - already agreed |
| Objection decision | החלטה בהשגה (צו) | Their decision on your objection | HIGH - 30 days to appeal to the District Court |
| Refund notice | הודעה על החזר | You are getting money back | LOW - informational |
| Debt notice | הודעת חוב | You owe money, pay up | HIGH - interest accruing |
| Inquiry letter | מכתב בירורים | They want more information from you | MEDIUM - respond within stated deadline |
| Withholding certificate | אישור ניכוי מס במקור | Tax withholding rate for your business | LOW - informational |
| Deficiency fine | קנס גירעון | Penalty for under-reporting income (s.191) | HIGH - treated as an assessment, so the 30-day השגה applies |
| Late-filing fine | קנס על אי הגשת דו"ח | Fixed penalty per month of delay (s.188) | MEDIUM - read the letter for the review route; do not assume the 30-day השגה applies, and get professional advice |

**What they usually want:** Money, documents proving your income/expenses, or for you to file returns you have not filed yet. The Tax Authority is the most deadline-sensitive body. Missing a 30-day window often means losing the right to object.

**Contact:** see the Contact Us page on the Israel Tax Authority gov.il department page (`gov.il/he/departments/israel_tax_authority`), which also links the personal area ("אזור אישי ברשות המסים").

## National Insurance Institute (המוסד לביטוח לאומי)

**Also known as:** Bituach Leumi, HaMossad LeBituach Leumi, NII

**Letterhead identifiers:**
- "המוסד לביטוח לאומי" with the Bituach Leumi logo (menorah-style symbol)
- Branch name (e.g., "סניף תל אביב", "סניף ירושלים")
- Division name for specific benefits

**Reference number patterns:**
- ID number based (9-digit teudat zehut)
- Claim number: "תביעה מס'" + number
- Payment reference: "אסמכתא" + number

**Common document types:**

| Document | Hebrew Name | What It Means | Urgency |
|----------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| Benefit approval | אישור תביעה / אישור גמלה | Your claim was approved | LOW - good news |
| Benefit denial | דחיית תביעה | Your claim was denied | HIGH - 12 months to appeal to the Labor Court (6 months for alimony decisions) |
| Partial approval | אישור חלקי | Approved but not full amount | MEDIUM - review and decide if to appeal |
| Reserve duty payment | אישור תגמולי מילואים | Your miluim compensation details | LOW - payment incoming |
| Debt collection | דרישת תשלום חוב | You owe them money (overpayment, missed payments) | HIGH - interest accruing |
| Disability percentage | קביעת אחוזי נכות | Medical committee decision on disability rating | HIGH if you disagree - ערר within 60 days (general disability) or 30 days (work injury) |
| Child allowance update | עדכון קצבת ילדים | Change in your child benefit amount | LOW - informational |
| Maternity allowance | דמי לידה | Maternity/paternity benefit details | LOW - payment incoming |
| Unemployment benefits | דמי אבטלה | Unemployment benefit decision | MEDIUM - may require action |

**What they usually want:** To inform you of a decision, request additional medical documents, or notify you of a debt. Bituach Leumi letters are often dense with legal references but many are informational. The key question is always: "approved" or "denied."

**Contact:** national call centre *6050 or 04-8812345. Online at btl.gov.il.

## Municipalities (עיריות / מועצות מקומיות)

**Also known as:** Iriya, Moatza Mekomit, Local Authority

**Letterhead identifiers:**
- City/town emblem and name
- Department name: "אגף הגבייה" (collection), "מחלקת הארנונה" (arnona), "מחלקת רישוי" (licensing)

**Reference number patterns:**
- Property account number ("מס' חשבון נכס")
- Arnona account number
- Business license number

**Common document types:**

| Document | Hebrew Name | What It Means | Urgency |
|----------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| Arnona bill | שובר ארנונה | Your property tax bill | HIGH - pay by due date |
| Arnona discount decision | החלטת ועדת הנחות | Decision on your discount request | MEDIUM - may need to reapply |
| Property reclassification | שינוי סיווג נכס | They changed your property classification (affects rate) | HIGH - 90 days to object |
| Building violation | הודעה על חריגת בנייה | They detected unauthorized construction | HIGH - legal consequences |
| Business license | רישיון עסק | Business license approval/renewal/denial | HIGH if denial |
| Water/sewage bill | חשבון מים/ביוב | Utility bill | MEDIUM - pay by due date |
| Zoning notice | הודעה על תוכנית בניין עיר | Zoning/planning change in your area | MEDIUM - right to object within stated period |
| Debt warning | התראה לפני נקיטת הליכים | Final warning before legal action | URGENT - act immediately |

**What they usually want:** Payment, compliance with building regulations, or to inform you of changes affecting your property. Municipalities are the most common source of "scary looking" letters that turn out to be routine bills.

**Contact:** Varies by municipality. Check your city's website for the relevant department.

## Interior Ministry (משרד הפנים)

**Also known as:** Misrad HaPnim

> Most letters a citizen gets about ID cards, passports, births, marriages or name changes come from the **Population and Immigration Authority** (PIBA / רשות האוכלוסין וההגירה), a unit of the Interior Ministry that runs the population registry as well as immigration, citizenship, residency, visas and work permits. Letters headed "משרד הפנים" itself are more often about local-government oversight or planning. If the letterhead says "רשות האוכלוסין וההגירה", see the PIBA section below.

**Letterhead identifiers:**
- "משרד הפנים" with the ministry emblem
- May show specific bureau: "לשכת רישום" (registration office), local government oversight unit, "מינהל אוכלוסין" for citizen identity functions

**Reference number patterns:**
- Application number ("בקשה מס'")
- ID number (teudat zehut)

**Common document types:**

| Document | Hebrew Name | What It Means | Urgency |
|----------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| Local-government notice | הודעת אגף השלטון המקומי | Ministry oversight of municipal matters | MEDIUM |

**What they usually want:** Usually nothing from an individual; most personal letters bearing the ministry name are PIBA letters (below).

**Online:** `gov.il/he/departments/ministry_of_interior`. Personal area at `gov.il` after sign-in.

## Population and Immigration Authority (רשות האוכלוסין וההגירה / PIBA)

**Also known as:** Rashut Ha'ochlosin VeHahagira, PIBA

**Letterhead identifiers:**
- "רשות האוכלוסין וההגירה"
- Specific bureau or regional office name (Jerusalem, Tel Aviv, Beer Sheva, etc.)

**Reference number patterns:**
- Application number ("בקשה מס'")
- Visa / permit number
- For visa holders: passport-linked file number rather than teudat zehut

**Common document types:**

| Document | Hebrew Name | What It Means | Urgency |
|----------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| ID card / passport notice | הודעה בעניין תעודת זהות / דרכון | Renewal, pickup or biometric update | MEDIUM |
| Population-registry notice | רישום לידה / נישואין / שינוי שם | A civil-status change was registered | LOW |
| Status decision | החלטה בעניין מעמד | Decision on residency/citizenship status | HIGH - ערר to the Appeals Tribunal (בית הדין לעררים) within 30 days of the decision or of learning of it, whichever is earlier; then appeal to the Administrative Affairs Court within 45 days |
| Visa extension | הארכת אשרה | Visa extension approval or denial (work, tourist, temporary-resident and other visa classes) | HIGH - affects legal stay |
| Family reunification decision | החלטה בעניין איחוד משפחות | Decision on a spouse/family residency application | HIGH - appeal windows apply |
| Biometric appointment | זימון לטביעות אצבע | Appointment for biometrics | MEDIUM - must attend on date |
| Work-permit notice | אישור / סירוב היתר עבודה | Decision on a work permit for a foreign worker | HIGH |

**What they usually want:** Additional documents to process your application, notification of a decision, or for you to attend a biometric / interview appointment.

**Online:** `gov.il/he/departments/population_and_immigration_authority`, with a "לאזור האישי" link. In-person appointments through `govisit.gov.il`.

## Land Registry (לשכת רישום המקרקעין / טאבו)

**Also known as:** Tabu, Land Registration Office

**Letterhead identifiers:**
- "לשכת רישום המקרקעין", often with a "משרד המשפטים" header
- Regional office name

**Reference number patterns:**
- Block/Parcel numbers ("גוש" / "חלקה" / "תת-חלקה")
- Registration file number

**Common document types:**

| Document | Hebrew Name | What It Means | Urgency |
|----------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| Registration confirmation | אישור רישום | Property transaction registered | LOW - good news |
| Lien notice | הודעת עיקול | A lien was placed on your property | URGENT |
| Caveat notice | הודעת הערת אזהרה | A caveat was registered on your property | HIGH - investigate |
| Correction notice | הודעת תיקון | Correction to registry records | MEDIUM |
| Missing documents | דרישת השלמת מסמכים | They need more documents to complete registration | MEDIUM |
| Registrar decision | החלטת רשם המקרקעין | A registration request was refused or decided against you | URGENT - ערר to the Registrar Supervisor (הממונה על המרשם) within 15 days of delivery or of learning of it; then District Court within 15 days |

**What they usually want:** Documents to complete a property registration, or to notify you of changes to your property's legal status.

## Courts (בתי משפט)

**Also known as:** Beit Mishpat, Beit Din (rabbinical)

**Letterhead identifiers:**
- State emblem + "בית משפט" + court type + location
- Court types: שלום (Magistrate), מחוזי (District), עליון (Supreme), תביעות קטנות (Small Claims), עבודה (Labor), משפחה (Family)

**Reference number patterns:**
- Case number format: ת"א (civil), ת"פ (criminal), ת"ק (small claims), תמ"ש (family)
- Year + sequential number (e.g., "ת"א 12345-01-26")

**Common document types:**

| Document | Hebrew Name | What It Means | Urgency |
|----------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| Summons | הזמנה לדין | You are called to court (as plaintiff, defendant, or witness) | URGENT |
| Claim | כתב תביעה | Someone is suing you | URGENT - file defense |
| Judgment | פסק דין | Court's final decision | HIGH - appeal window |
| Interim order | צו ביניים | Temporary court order | URGENT - must comply |
| Hearing notice | הודעה על מועד דיון | Date for your next hearing | HIGH - must attend |
| Execution order | צו הוצאה לפועל | Court-ordered enforcement (debt collection) | URGENT |

**What they usually want:** Your appearance, your written response, or compliance with an order. Court documents are ALWAYS urgent and should be treated with maximum priority.

## Enforcement and Collection Authority (רשות האכיפה והגבייה / הוצאה לפועל)

**Also known as:** Hotzaa LaPoal, Execution Office

**Letterhead identifiers:**
- "רשות האכיפה והגבייה" or "לשכת ההוצאה לפועל"

**Reference number patterns:**
- Execution file number ("תיק הוצל"פ")

**Common document types:**

| Document | Hebrew Name | What It Means | Urgency |
|----------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| Debt notice | אזהרה | You have an open enforcement file against you | URGENT - 20 days on a judgment file; 30 days to object on a bill, cheque or fixed-sum claim file |
| Property seizure | עיקול | Your assets/bank account has been frozen | URGENT |
| Payment arrangement | הסדר תשלומים | Terms for paying off debt | HIGH - comply or renegotiate |
| Restriction on leaving country | צו עיכוב יציאה מהארץ | You cannot leave Israel until debt resolved | URGENT |

**What they usually want:** Payment. Hotzaa LaPoal is the most aggressive collection mechanism in Israel. Their letters should always be treated as urgent. What an "אזהרה" (warning) allows depends on the file:

- **Judgment file** (the debt comes from a court judgment): 20 days from service to pay, arrange instalments, or ask the registrar for a payment order (Execution Law s.7(a)). There is no opposition: the judgment itself is not re-litigated here.
- **Bill or cheque file** (שטר): 30 days from service to file an opposition (התנגדות), which stays execution and moves the dispute to court (s.81א).
- **Fixed-sum claim file** (תביעה על סכום קצוב, a contractual or statutory debt of up to 75,000 NIS filed directly at the Execution Office): also 30 days from service of the אזהרה to file an opposition (s.81א1).

Ignoring it leads to seizures and restrictions such as a stay-of-exit order.

### Administrative seizure (עיקול מנהלי)

Not every seizure comes from Hotzaa LaPoal. Municipalities and other public bodies that collect under the Taxes Ordinance (Collection) (פקודת המסים (גבייה)) may seize a debtor's movable property without any court order, once the debt has gone unpaid for 15 days from its due date and a written demand was sent. A seizure letter from a municipality or a public body is therefore not proof that a court case exists. The route to contest one depends on the creditor, so recommend professional advice promptly.

## State Comptroller and Ombudsman (מבקר המדינה ונציב תלונות הציבור)

**Also known as:** Mevaker HaMedina, Netziv Tlunot HaTzibur

**Role:** Independent constitutional body that audits public bodies and handles complaints from the public against government offices, municipalities, and state-owned entities. Generally the **next escalation step after standard objection / appeal channels with the agency itself have failed** (or are clearly futile) and before considering an administrative petition to the courts.

**Letterhead identifiers:**
- "משרד מבקר המדינה ונציב תלונות הציבור"

**When to direct a user here:**
- Standard objection (השגה) was rejected and internal appeal exhausted
- The user believes the agency acted improperly, not just incorrectly
- The complaint is about service quality, delay, abuse of authority, or maladministration

**Online:** complaint intake at `complaint.mevaker.gov.il` (the descriptive landing page is `mevaker.gov.il/contact-us/הגשת-תלונה`). In Israel the State Comptroller (מבקר המדינה) and the Ombudsman (נציב תלונות הציבור) are the same office.

## Freedom of Information Requests (חוק חופש המידע, התשנ"ח-1998)

When a government letter cites information (a report, a calculation, an internal memo) that the user cannot otherwise access, the user has a statutory right to request it under the **Freedom of Information Law, 5758-1998** (חוק חופש המידע).

- Each public body has a designated "Information Officer" (ממונה על חוק חופש המידע)
- A request fee of 24 NIS applies, plus a per-hour handling charge from the 4th hour of work (36 NIS per hour in the indexed fees regulations) and per-page reproduction charge, with exemptions for certain categories
- The body must respond within 30 days; it may extend by 30 more, and for a voluminous or complex request by up to 60 more with written reasons, so 120 days at most
- Refusal can be appealed administratively and then to court

**Service page:** `gov.il/he/service/freedom_of_information_submission`. Verify the current fee schedule before quoting numbers to a user, since the fees regulations are updated periodically.

## Administrative Petition / Bagatz (עתירה מנהלית / בג"ץ)

**Last-resort judicial review** of decisions by a minister, ministry, or other state authority:
- **Administrative petition** (עתירה מנהלית) is filed at the District Court sitting as an Administrative Affairs Court (בית משפט לעניינים מנהליים) for most ministry decisions. Strict time limits apply (typically 45 days from the decision, sometimes shorter).
- **Bagatz** (the High Court of Justice / בית המשפט הגבוה לצדק, a hat of the Supreme Court) hears petitions that fall outside the administrative-courts jurisdiction or that raise constitutional / nationwide-impact issues.

This is a step for situations where every administrative remedy inside the agency and its parent ministry has been exhausted (or is plainly futile) and the user is willing to incur legal-representation cost. Always recommend consulting a lawyer before drafting such a petition. Which court hears a given petition depends on its subject matter, so this is a question for the lawyer.

## General Tips for Identifying Unknown Senders

1. **Check the return address** on the envelope, not just the letter
2. **Look for a "פרטי מענה"** (response details) section, which often names the department
3. **Google the phone number** on the letter to identify the office
4. **Reference number format** is often the strongest clue (each body uses distinct patterns)
5. **Legal citations** point to the administering body (e.g., "חוק הביטוח הלאומי" = Bituach Leumi)
6. **Collection notices** may come from private companies acting on behalf of a government body, always look for the original creditor name
