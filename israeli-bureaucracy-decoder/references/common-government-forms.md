# Common Israeli Government Forms

An index of frequently encountered government forms, what they are for, who needs to file them, and when. Use this when a document references a form number or when advising which forms to file.

---

## Tax Authority Forms (טפסי רשות המסים)

### Income Tax Forms

| Form | Hebrew Name | Purpose | Who Files | When |
|------|-------------|---------|-----------|------|
| **101** | טופס 101 | Employee card and request for tax relief by the employer (כרטיס עובד ובקשה להקלה ולתיאום מס על ידי המעביד): personal details and credit points | Every employee | Within 7 days of starting a job, and again at the start of every tax year |
| **106** | טופס 106 | Annual employer tax certificate: summary of your salary and tax withheld during the year | Employer gives to employee | By March 31 of the following year |
| **135** | טופס 135 | Short annual report (דין וחשבון שנתי מקוצר), used mainly to request a tax refund | Individuals who are not a company and are NOT required to file an annual return (typically salaried employees claiming a refund) | Up to 6 years back (Income Tax Ordinance s.160): in 2026, refunds for 2020-2025 |
| **1301** | טופס 1301 | Annual income return (דו"ח הכנסות) for individuals who must file. Common annexes include 1320 (profit and loss for the self-employed), 1321, 1324 (foreign income), 1342 (depreciation), 1344 (carried-forward losses), 1348 (residency) and 6111 (financial-statement coding) | Self-employed, business owners, others required to file | See deadline note below (tax year 2025 reporting in 2026: May 29, 2026 paper / June 30, 2026 online per gov.il) |
| **1214** | טופס 1214 | Company annual return (דין וחשבון על הכנסת החברה). NOT a refund request for employees | Companies | Per the companies' annual deadline (tax year 2025: July 31, 2026) |
| **7000** | טופס 7000 | Taxes on Land declaration (הצהרה) on a sale or purchase of real estate, which starts the betterment-tax (מס שבח) and purchase-tax assessment | Seller and buyer | Within 30 days of the sale (Real Estate Taxation Law s.73(a)) |
| **119** | טופס 119 | Tax benefit for completing an academic degree or vocational qualification (בקשה להטבה במס לזכאים לתואר אקדמי או לתעודת מקצוע) | Graduates claiming the study credit | With the annual return for the relevant years |
| **134** | טופס 134 | Computations following excess employer contributions to a keren hishtalmut or provident fund (annex to 1301 or 135) | Employees whose employer contributed above the exempt ceilings | With the annual return |
| **116** | טופס 116 | Tax coordination (tium mas) when you have two or more employers: the ITA online tax-coordination service takes a completed Form 116 plus supporting documents. Pensioners on pension income only may use the shorter Form 101ג | Employees with multiple jobs | When starting an additional job |
| **(no form)** | הודעת השגה | Objection to a tax assessment (hashagah). NOT a numbered form: an objection is filed as a free-form written notice ("הודעת השגה") stating the grounds, under Section 150 of the Income Tax Ordinance (סעיף 150 לפקודת מס הכנסה). "150" is the law section, not a form number | Anyone who disagrees with their assessment | Within 30 days of receiving the assessment |
| **150** | טופס 150 | Declaration of holdings in a foreign corporation (הצהרה על החזקה בחבר בני אדם תושב חוץ). This is the real ITA Form 150, filed with the annual return by Israeli residents holding interests in foreign entities. It is UNRELATED to tax objections | Israeli residents holding interests in foreign corporations | Annually, with the income tax return |

> **Annual return deadline note (form 1301):** The Tax Authority extends these annual filing deadlines almost every year. For tax year 2025 (reported in 2026), gov.il published deadlines are **May 29, 2026 (paper)** and **June 30, 2026 (online)** per the gov.il service page `gov.il/he/service/reporting-and-payment-2025-annual-tax-report-for-individuals`. Earlier guidance of "April 30 (paper) / May 31 (online)" is the historical default. Always verify the exact current dates on gov.il before advising a user, since the Tax Authority can issue additional extensions during the filing season.

### VAT Forms

| Form | Hebrew Name | Purpose | Who Files | When |
|------|-------------|---------|-----------|------|
| **821** | טופס 821 | Application to open a VAT file as an osek murshe (VAT registration). Partnerships and companies file 821 together with 821א | New businesses | Before starting business activity |
| Periodic VAT report | דוח תקופתי למע"מ | The monthly or bi-monthly VAT return and payment | VAT-registered businesses | By the 15th of the following month, or the 19th when filed and paid online |
| Detailed VAT report | דיווח מפורט | Invoice-level report required from larger dealers | Dealers required to file it | Online, by the 23rd of the month |
| Osek patur annual declaration | הצהרה שנתית של עוסק פטור | Declaration of the exempt dealer's turnover in the past tax year | Every osek patur | Once a year; check the current date on gov.il, since the Tax Authority has extended it in recent years |

## National Insurance (Bituach Leumi) Forms (טפסי ביטוח לאומי)

| Form | Hebrew Name | Purpose | Who Files | When |
|------|-------------|---------|-----------|------|
| **BL/502** | בל/502 | Personal claim for reserve-duty compensation (תביעה אישית לתגמולי מילואים), with IDF form 3010 attached | Reservists who are not monthly salaried employees. Salaried employees are paid through the employer; the self-employed are usually paid automatically from IDF data | Self-employed: only if not paid within 3 weeks of the end of service |
| **BL/211** | בל/211 | Claim for work-injury pay and notice of a work injury (תביעה לתשלום דמי פגיעה והודעה על פגיעה בעבודה) | Employees and self-employed injured at work | Within 12 months of the injury |
| **BL/355** | בל/355 | Maternity allowance claim (דמי לידה), form 360 for a father | Usually not needed: payment is triggered by the hospital and employer data | Within 12 months of the day work stopped, or the right may be lost; online only after the birth |
| **BL/7801** | בל/7801 | General disability pension claim (קצבת נכות כללית) | Insured persons over 18 whose earning capacity was lost or reduced by 50% or more | As soon as the condition affects earning capacity |
| **BL/1500** | בל/1500 | Unemployment benefits claim (דמי אבטלה); employer certificate is form 1514 | Recently unemployed individuals | Within 12 months of first reporting to the Employment Service |
| **BL/5025** | בל/5025 | Personal claim for child allowance | Usually not needed: paid automatically about a month after the birth. Use it when not receiving the allowance or when a child moves between parents | When the allowance is not paid automatically |
| **BL/480** | בל/480 | Old-age pension claim (קצבת אזרח ותיק) | People reaching retirement age | BTL mails a pre-filled claim about a month before retirement age |
| **BL/580** | בל/580 | Notice of injury in hostile action and claim for recognition as a victim (נפגעי פעולות איבה) | Victims of terror and hostile action | As soon as possible |

Most of these claims can now be filed online on `btl.gov.il`, so the form number matters mainly for recognising a letter that cites it.

## Municipality Forms (טפסים עירוניים)

| Form | Purpose | Who Files | When |
|------|---------|-----------|------|
| **Arnona discount request** (בקשה להנחה בארנונה) | Apply for reduced arnona based on income, age, disability, new immigrant status, or other qualifying criteria | Property owners/renters meeting eligibility criteria | Per the municipality's annual application window |
| **Property usage change notification** (הודעה על שינוי שימוש בנכס) | Notify the municipality that the use of your property changed (e.g., from residential to commercial) | Property owners | Promptly after the change; the notice period is set in the municipality's arnona order |
| **Business license application** (בקשה לרישיון עסק) | Apply for a license to operate a business | Anyone opening or relocating a business | Before starting operations |
| **Building permit application** (בקשה להיתר בנייה) | Request permission to build or renovate | Property owners planning construction | Before starting any construction work |
| **Objection to arnona classification** (השגה על סיווג ארנונה) | Challenge how the municipality classified your property (which affects your rate) | Property owners who believe classification is wrong | Within 90 days of receiving the arnona bill |

## Population and Immigration Authority Forms (טפסי רשות האוכלוסין וההגירה)

ID cards, passports and population-registry changes for citizens are handled by the Population and Immigration Authority (a unit of the Interior Ministry), not by the ministry's own offices. Book in-person service through `govisit.gov.il`.


| Form | Purpose | Who Files | When |
|------|---------|-----------|------|
| **ID card application** (בקשה לתעודת זהות) | Apply for or renew an Israeli ID card | Citizens getting a first card, or replacing a lost or damaged one | When first needed, or upon change of status |
| **Passport application** (בקשה לדרכון) | Apply for or renew an Israeli passport | Israeli citizens | When passport expires or is needed for the first time |
| **Name change request** (בקשה לשינוי שם) | Legally change your name | Israeli citizens | Anytime |
| **Visa extension request** (בקשה להארכת אשרה) | Extend a visa/residence permit | Non-citizens in Israel | Before the current visa expires |
| **Family reunification application** (בקשה לאיחוד משפחות) | Apply for a spouse or family member to receive residency | Israeli citizens/residents with foreign family members | Varies |

## Land Registry (Tabu) Forms (טפסי טאבו)

| Form | Purpose | Who Files | When |
|------|---------|-----------|------|
| **Property transfer request** (בקשה להעברת זכויות) | Transfer property ownership from one person to another | Buyer and seller of property | After completing a real estate transaction |
| **Mortgage registration** (בקשה לרישום משכנתא) | Register a mortgage against a property | Banks/lending institutions | When a mortgage is taken |
| **Caveat registration** (בקשה לרישום הערת אזהרה) | Register a warning note on a property (e.g., during a purchase process) | Buyers, creditors | During property transactions or when establishing a legal claim |
| **Nesach Tabu request** (בקשה לנסח טאבו) | Request an official extract showing property ownership and encumbrances | Anyone (it is public information) | Available online at gov.il for a small fee |

## Court Forms (טפסי בית משפט)

| Form | Purpose | Who Files | When |
|------|---------|-----------|------|
| **Statement of claim** (כתב תביעה) | Initiate a lawsuit | The plaintiff (person suing) | When filing a case |
| **Statement of defense** (כתב הגנה) | Respond to a lawsuit | The defendant (person being sued) | **Magistrate Court (שלום): 60 days** from service of the claim under תקנות סדר הדין האזרחי, תשע"ט-2018 (Civil Procedure Regulations 2018). **Small Claims (תביעות קטנות): 30 days** from service of the claim (per kolzchut "הגשת תביעה קטנה"; this derives from the small-claims regime, not the general 60-day civil-procedure rule). Some specialized proceedings (e.g. the State as a party) may set their own period in the summons, so always verify on the court summons itself |
| **Small claims form** (טופס תביעה קטנה) | File a small claims case (up to **39,900 NIS as of January 2026** per kolzchut "הגשת תביעה קטנה"; the ceiling is updated periodically by regulation) | Anyone suing for a small amount | When filing at Small Claims Court |
| **Fee exemption request** (בקשה לפטור מאגרה) | Request waiver of court filing fees due to financial hardship | Low-income plaintiffs | When filing the case |
| **Opposition to execution** (התנגדות לביצוע) | Challenge execution of a bill or cheque (שטר) or a fixed-sum claim (תביעה על סכום קצוב) | Debtors on a bill, cheque or fixed-sum claim file at Hotzaa LaPoal | Within 30 days of service of the warning (azhara). On a judgment file there is no opposition: the debtor has 20 days to pay, arrange instalments or ask for a payment order |

## Tips for Form Filing

1. **Always keep copies** of every form you submit, with the date stamp or receipt confirmation.
2. **Online filing** is available for many forms at gov.il, the Tax Authority personal area (linked from its gov.il page), and Bituach Leumi portal (btl.gov.il). Online filing is generally faster and provides automatic confirmation. Many digital forms now require a **digital signature** (comsign / PersonalID), see the digital identification section below.
3. **Deadlines matter.** Most government forms have strict deadlines. Late submissions may be accepted with penalties, or may not be accepted at all.
4. **Hebrew is required** for most government forms. Some forms are available in Arabic and English, but the Hebrew version is always the authoritative one.
5. **Notarization (notaryon)** is required for some forms, especially those involving property or powers of attorney.
6. **Apostille**: for a foreign document submitted in Israel, ask the receiving office what authentication it requires before the user travels or orders copies. An Israeli apostille is for Israeli documents going abroad: the Foreign Ministry issues it for government certificates (electronically on gov.il for some of them) and the courts for court and notarial documents.

## Digital Identification and Personal Area (אזור אישי / MyGov)

Most Israeli government portals now require a verified digital identity before you can view letters, submit forms, or pay. The unified government sign-in is `login.gov.il`. The most common personal-area ("אזור אישי") accounts are:

| Body | Personal Area URL | What you can see/do |
|------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Tax Authority | "אזור אישי ברשות המסים" on the Tax Authority's gov.il page (opens `secapp.taxes.gov.il/sr-ezor-ishi`) | Returns, assessments, refunds, withholding certificates, debt status |
| Bituach Leumi | `btl.gov.il` (personal area / "אזור אישי") | Claim status, payment history, dmei miluim / dmei leida / unemployment, child allowance |
| Population and Immigration Authority (PIBA) | `gov.il/he/departments/population_and_immigration_authority` ("לאזור האישי" link on the page) | Visa status, ID card and passport applications, residency files |
| Unified gov.il | `gov.il` after sign-in | Cross-ministry forms, appointment booking |

**Digital signature:** Some forms (especially in tax, courts, and property contexts) require a personal digital signature certificate from a certification authority (גורם מאשר) recognised by the Ministry of Justice under the Electronic Signature Law, 5761-2001. Two companies are recognised: Comsign and Personal ID (gov.il notary guidance on electronic notarial certificates).

> Before escalating to a phone call or office visit, **check the relevant personal area first**. Many letters that look "missing" are actually sitting in the user's digital inbox.

## Appointment Booking (זימון תור / iruim makdimim)

Most government offices now require a pre-booked appointment for in-person service. The unified gov.il portal is **`govisit.gov.il`**. It books, among others:

- **Population and Immigration Authority (PIBA):** ID cards, passports, population-registry changes, citizenship.
- **Ministry of Transport** and **court-system** services.

The Tax Authority links its appointment booking ("זימון תור") from its gov.il page. For Bituach Leumi, check `btl.gov.il` for how its branches take appointments.

Walking in without an appointment is usually refused. Confirm available time slots before recommending a physical visit, and check the office's reception hours on its gov.il page.
