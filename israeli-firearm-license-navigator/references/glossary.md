# Glossary: Hebrew Terms of the Firearm License Process

Transliteration follows common Israeli usage. Use the Hebrew term in Hebrew output and the transliteration in parentheses in English output.

## Bodies and people

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|-----------------|---------|
| המשרד לביטחון לאומי | ha-misrad le-vitachon leumi | Ministry of National Security (formerly Ministry of Public Security, misrad le-vitachon pnim); the licensing authority sits here |
| האגף לרישוי כלי ירייה | ha-agaf le-rishui klei yeriya | Firearms Licensing Department (also "אגף רישוי ופיקוח כלי ירייה", licensing and supervision) |
| פקיד רישוי | pakid rishui | Licensing official; the person who decides on the application |
| הרשות המוסמכת | ha-rashut ha-musmechet | The competent authority under the Law: the licensing official acting on a senior police officer's recommendation |
| הממונה | ha-memuneh | The supervisor who decides appeals |
| לשכת רישוי מחוזית | lishkat rishui mechozit | District licensing bureau (six districts: North in Afula, Haifa, Tel Aviv, Jerusalem, Centre in Ramla, South in Beer Sheva) |
| מרכז שירות ומידע | merkaz sherut u-meida | Service and information center, *8657 |
| מטווח מורשה | mitvach mursheh | Authorised shooting range |
| מדריך ירי | madrich yeri | Licensed shooting instructor |
| בית מסחר / סוחר מורשה | beit mischar / socher mursheh | Licensed firearm dealer |
| קצין רישוי כלי ירייה במשטרה | ktzin rishui klei yeriya | Police firearms licensing officer |

## Documents

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|-----------------|---------|
| רישיון פרטי | rishayon prati | Private license to carry, transport, or hold a firearm |
| אישור מותנה | ishur mutne | Conditional approval issued after the interview; users often say "rishayon mutne"; valid half a year per the Knesset review; not a license |
| רישיון זמני | rishayon zmani | Temporary paper license from the dealer or range, valid until the card arrives |
| רישיון מגנטי / כרטיס רישיון קבוע | rishayon magneti | The plastic card, mailed within 90 days |
| הצהרת בריאות | hatzharat briut | Health declaration signed by the applicant and a physician |
| ספח תעודת זהות | sefach | The appendix of the identity card |
| אישור שירות | ishur sherut | Confirmation of military, national or civil service, or exemption |
| תצהיר | tatzhir | Affidavit; gov.il publishes one per criterion |
| שטר מכר | shtar mecher | Bill of sale for a firearm or ammunition |
| כתב התחייבות | ktav hitchayvut | Undertaking, for example the safe undertaking or the ammunition top-up undertaking |
| אישור תשלום אגרה | ishur tashlum agra | Fee payment confirmation |
| אישור ביצוע הכשרה | ishur bitzua hachshara | Training confirmation from the range |
| טופס ערר | tofes arar | Appeal form, signed by a lawyer |
| הודעת חידוש | hodaat chidush | Renewal notice |
| תעודת הרשאה | teudat harsha'a | Permit to hold an organisational firearm; not a private license |

## Process terms

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|-----------------|---------|
| תנאי סף | tnai saf | Threshold conditions, all required |
| תבחין | tavchin | Criterion, one required from the closed list |
| תבחין משמר | tavchin meshamer | Grandfathered basis for holders who predate the current criteria; not valid for a replacement firearm |
| מחשבון זכאות | machshevon zakaut | The official eligibility calculator |
| מערכת ההזדהות הלאומית | maarechet ha-hizdahut ha-leumit | National identification system used to sign in to gov.il forms |
| אזור אישי | ezor ishi | Personal area on gov.il, where forms and status live |
| ראיון טלפוני | reayon telefoni | Phone interview; some criteria are exempt |
| הכשרה ראשונית | hachshara rishonit | Initial training, 4.5 hours |
| הכשרה מקוצרת | hachshara mekutzeret | Shortened training, 2 hours, for prior experience |
| מבחן עיוני / מבחן מעשי | mivchan iyuni / mivchan maasi | Theory exam / practical qualification, 70 percent |
| הכשרת ריענון תקופתית | hachsharat rianun tkufatit | Periodic refresher in year 2 |
| חידוש רישיון | chidush rishayon | Renewal in year 3 |
| אגרה | agra | Fee; 71 NIS per year as of 04.01.2026 |
| תוספת אגרה | tosefet agra | Surcharge for late renewal |
| הפקדה | hafkada | Deposit of the firearm at a dealer or the police |
| ביטול רישיון | bitul rishayon | Cancellation |
| ערר | arar | Appeal to the supervisor within 45 days |
| שימוע | shimua | Hearing; users may ask for one before cancellation |
| כספת ביתית | kasefet beitit | Home safe meeting the gov.il specification |
| מכסת תחמושת | michsat tachmoshet | Ammunition quota under the license conditions |
| השלמת תחמושת | hashlamat tachmoshet | Ammunition top-up |
| הוראת שעה | horaat shaa | Temporary order, used for wartime extensions |
| חרבות ברזל | charvot barzel | Iron Swords, the war that began 7.10.2023; several arrangements reference it |
| משרת מילואים פעיל | mesharet miluim pail | Active reservist; 50 percent fee discount |
| יישוב זכאי / יישוב מזכה | yishuv zakai | Settlement the police recommended as qualifying under the residence criterion |
| רובאי 07 | rovai 07 | Rifleman training level cited in the security-forces criterion |
| תעודת לוחם | teudat lochem | Combat soldier certificate |
