## 1. The 3-year cycle

| Year | Event | Where | Fee |
|------|-------|-------|-----|
| 1 | Nothing scheduled; keep the paper license until the card arrives | | |
| 2 | Periodic refresher training; reminder sent at the start of year 2 by Israel Post and electronically | Authorised range | No license fee (range charges apply) |
| 3 | Renewal during year 3, completed before expiry; reminder sent from 3 months before | Authorised range, forms from the personal area | 71 NIS per year or part, 50 percent off for active reservists renewing on time |

gov.il: the license is valid for 3 years, renewal happens in the third year, and renewal is the holder's responsibility; the process must be completed before expiry.

## 2. Year 2: periodic refresher

gov.il refresher page:

- Who: holders of a private firearm license.
- When: in the second year of the license period. At the start of the second year a reminder is sent by mail and electronically.
- Content: theory part; practical part of 40 rounds followed by a 10-round qualification, pass mark 70 percent.
- Failing: additional refresher sessions are allowed within 30 days of the first refresher.
- Reservists: if the holder served 30 or more reserve days cumulatively in the 90 days before the last day for the refresher, that period is extended once by six months (Firearms (Training) Regulations, regulation 3A, added in 5785). gov.il (14.07.2025) says the department sends an SMS about the extension, and that until the end of the "special situation on the home front" declaration, career soldiers in a role at activity level A and above get the same relief.
- Bring: the printed refresher confirmation form, and a health declaration of the license holder to show the range manager; if details given in the application or the last renewal changed, attach an updated health declaration.
- After passing, the range signs the confirmation form and sends a copy to the licensing bureau. Carry the confirmation whenever carrying the firearm.

## 3. Year 3: renewal

gov.il renewal page:

1. From 3 months before expiry a reminder arrives about the renewal refresher. A chart on the page shows when a regular refresher and when a renewal refresher is due.
2. Sign in to the government personal area, open the "כלי ירייה" tab and click "להורדת הטפסים" to get the personal list of documents and the forms.
3. Pay the fee through the government payment service.
4. Go to an authorised range with the payment confirmation and the documents. A photo is taken at the range for the new card. Complete the renewal refresher.
5. After the refresher the range issues a temporary license signed by the range. The license is valid for 3 years.
6. The permanent plastic license is mailed to the address registered with the Population Authority, unless another address was given.

The criteria page adds that the health declaration and undertaking are signed on every renewal. Regulation 3(a) of the criteria regulations requires a criterion to hold on the day the renewal request is filed, except for the continuity route below.

Three routes to know before expiry:

- Extension on request (validity regulation 4): the licensing official may extend a license by up to 30 days on a reasoned request from the holder, and again by up to 30 days at a time, as long as the extensions total no more than 45 days.
- Reservist extension (validity regulation 4A, added in 5785): the license of a person who served 30 or more reserve days cumulatively in the 90 days before it expires is extended once by six months from the end of its validity. gov.il calls it automatic, says an SMS is sent, and says (14.07.2025) that until the end of the "special situation on the home front" declaration, career soldiers in a role at activity level A and above get the same relief for the license as well as the refresher. Whether that declaration is still in force on the day you check is not stated on the page; ask the service center.
- Reservist extension after a wartime order: when a temporary order (section 6) had already moved the expiry, the regulation does not say whether the 90 days and the 6 months count from the original date or from the order's date. The calculator asks about both windows and reports the period up to the later possible date as uncertain (no offence note, no surcharge) until the service center confirms.
- Continuity renewal (criteria regulation 10): a pistol held lawfully for 10 or more continuous years under criteria 1 to 9 may be renewed even without a current criterion, but only if the license is valid on the day the request is filed.

Active reservists: 50 percent off the license fee, provided the renewal was done by the set date and the eligibility card is shown.

## 4. Missing the refresher: the 72-hour rule

gov.il (both the refresher and renewal pages): a holder who did not complete the refresher on time must deposit the firearm at a licensed dealer within 72 hours of receiving the licensing official's notice of non-completion. A holder who does not deposit it will have the license cancelled and must deposit the firearm immediately at a police station. After cancellation, getting a license again requires a new application, the training program, and meeting the criteria in force at the time of the new application.

A firearm in custody at a dealer: Law section 14c (Kol Zchut, updated 1.2.2026, summarises the same rules) lets a holder give a firearm into a dealer's custody for a fixed period against custody fees set by order; if the license lapses while the firearm is in custody, it is returned only against a license; a year after the end of the custody period the dealer may sell it, after a registered letter at least 14 days before the sale, and pays the owner the proceeds less custody fees and commission, or may hand it to the police instead. Section 14c is written for a voluntary deposit for a fixed period; the sources do not say whether all of it governs a deposit forced by a missed refresher, so ask the service center.

## 5. After expiry: deposit, a new application, surcharge tiers

gov.il fee page, section "תוספת תשלום אגרה למי שלא חידש את רישיונו במועד":

- The license must be renewed before expiry. The fee page says a holder who did not renew on time must deposit the firearm at the Israel Police at once ("מיד"), because holding a firearm without a valid license is a criminal offence.
- The regulation sets the outer limit: the firearm, the license and the ammunition are deposited within 72 hours of expiry, or of receiving a cancellation notice (criteria regulation 9(a)), at the police station of the holder's residence or business, which issues a receipt (Law section 14). If they were not deposited and a renewal request is filed, the licensing official may refuse it for that reason (regulation 9(b)).
- A request filed after expiry is treated as a request for a new license (criteria regulation 8), so the criteria in force today apply and the continuity route is gone. Separately, validity regulation 5 lets the licensing official renew an expired license, if persuaded it is justified, provided the request was filed within 30 days of expiry. The regulations do not say how the two provisions fit together; file inside the 30 days if at all possible, and ask the service center how the file will be handled.
- After depositing, if renewal is approved, a surcharge is added to the renewal fee according to the delay:

| Delay after expiry | Surcharge |
|--------------------|-----------|
| Up to 6 months | The fee for one year of that license |
| 6 to 12 months | The fee for three years of that license |
| Over 12 months | For each year of delay or part of it, the fee for four years of that license |

The fee page notes the amounts are shown for convenience and the Firearms (License Fees) Regulations, 5766-2005, prevail.

Section 16(a)(1) of the Law sets a specific, lighter penalty, six months' imprisonment, for a breach of sections 4 or 5 that consists only of not renewing the license within one year of its expiry ("בשל כך בלבד שלא חידש את רישיונו ... בתוך שנה מיום שפג תוקפם"). Penal Law section 144(b1) takes such a non-renewal-only case out of the general weapons offences in section 144(a) and (b) (seven and ten years). Do not read the one-year mark as the point where exposure starts: the gov.il fee page calls holding without a valid license a criminal offence. How these provisions apply to a user's own case is a question for a lawyer.

Not renewing at all: within 18 months of expiry or cancellation (whichever came first), a former holder may ask to keep the firearm permanently deactivated as a memento, after the competent authority confirms the deactivation (criteria regulation 12). That is a separate procedure, outside this skill.

## 6. Extensions: on request, reservists, and past wartime orders

Two extensions are standing rules: the extension on request and the reservist extension (section 3). Everything below is a past wartime temporary order with fixed dates. They are HISTORY: the last extended deadline was 31.8.2026, and no later order was found on 28.09.2026. Do not assume an extension exists.

- 2023 and 2024 regulations (validity temporary orders of 26.10.2023 and 28.01.2024, training temporary orders of 31.10.2023 and 30.01.2024; Wikisource, and gov.il news 13.12.2023, updated 18.02.2024): each order covers deadlines falling in a stated period (licenses: from 26.10.2023 to 31.12.2023 and from 31.01.2024 to 31.03.2024; refreshers: from 31.10.2023 to 31.12.2023 and from 01.01.2024 to 31.03.2024) and then names an extended date for each month-end only: 31.10.2023 to 30.04.2024, 30.11.2023 to 31.05.2024, 31.12.2023 to 30.06.2024, 31.01.2024 to 31.07.2024, 29.02.2024 to 31.08.2024, 31.03.2024 to 30.09.2024. Deadlines from 1 to 30 January 2024 fall outside both license periods. Conditional approvals issued June to December 2023 with an active application were extended by three months from their original expiry (gov.il).
- 21.05.2024 (gov.il private-license topic page, news item): holders whose license expired 31.5.2024 could request a 30-day extension by 26.5.2024 through the individual notice, with no further extensions.
- June and July 2025, after operation "Am KeLavi" (gov.il news 30.06.2025 and 14.07.2025): renewals and refreshers due 30.6.2025 and 31.7.2025 were extended to 30.9.2025 and 31.10.2025. Expired.
- 23.04.2026 (gov.il news, updated 26.04.2026): the National Security Committee approved a temporary order; refreshers and renewals due 31.3.2026, 30.4.2026 and 31.5.2026 were extended to 30.6.2026, 31.7.2026 and 31.8.2026 respectively. Expired on 31.8.2026.

Every one of these texts names an extended date for month-end dates only. The 2023 and 2024 regulations add a covered period around them; the 2025 and 2026 gov.il notices speak of holders who "were due to renew by" the month-end date, which reads naturally as the whole month but does not say so. Whether a deadline on another day inside a covered period or month was extended, and to when, is therefore not stated. The script applies an order to a named date, reports any other date in a covered period or month as "possibly covered" (status "uncertain" until the possible date passes, then both surcharge tiers), and sends the user to the service center.

## 7. License copy and cards that never arrived

gov.il "הוצאת העתק לרישיון":

- For a valid license that was stolen, lost, worn or never received; private, organisational, instructor and range-supervisor licenses.
- Pay online (fee table, 71 NIS as of 04.01.2026), then apply online or at a self-service kiosk.
- A permanent card is sent within 90 days of the request to the address on file at the department. Until then print the temporary paper license after applying.
- If 90 days passed since the license was produced and it did not arrive, and less than 6 months passed and the address did not change, call the service center to check eligibility for a free card.

## 8. Updating address and contact details

Two registries matter:

1. The department's own contact details, used for reminders, SMS and the card: update through the department's contact-update form at https://auth.govforms.gov.il/mw/forms/form19@mops.gov.il . The gov.il license-copy page links to it under the text "לעדכון פרטי התקשרות באגף לרישוי כלי ירייה" (link read in a browser on 14.09.2026; the form itself sits behind the national identification login).
2. The Population Authority address, used for the plastic card after renewal unless another address was given.

A move or job change that ends the criterion is not only an address update: the holder must report it to the licensing official without delay (criteria regulation 3(d)). See `eligibility-criteria.md`, section 7.

Emergency notices are sent to the email and mobile on file; the 13.12.2023 notice states that keeping them current is the holder's responsibility.
