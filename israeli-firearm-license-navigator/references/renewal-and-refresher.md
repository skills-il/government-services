## 1. The 3-year cycle

| Year | Event | Where | Fee |
|------|-------|-------|-----|
| 1 | Nothing scheduled; keep the paper license until the card arrives | | |
| 2 | Periodic refresher training; reminder sent at the start of year 2 by Israel Post and electronically | Authorised range | No license fee (range charges apply) |
| 3 | Renewal, from 3 months before expiry until expiry; reminder sent 3 months before | Authorised range, forms from the personal area | 71 NIS per year or part, 50 percent off for active reservists renewing on time |

gov.il: the license is valid for 3 years, renewal happens in the third year, and renewal is the holder's responsibility; the process must be completed before expiry.

## 2. Year 2: periodic refresher

gov.il refresher page:

- Who: holders of a private firearm license.
- When: in the second year of the license period. At the start of the second year a reminder is sent by mail and electronically.
- Content: theory part; practical part of 40 rounds followed by a 10-round qualification, pass mark 70 percent.
- Failing: additional refresher sessions are allowed within 30 days of the first refresher.
- Bring: the printed refresher confirmation form, and a health declaration of the license holder to show the range manager; if details given in the application or the last renewal changed, attach an updated health declaration.
- After passing, the range signs the confirmation form and sends a copy to the licensing bureau. Carry the confirmation whenever carrying the firearm.

## 3. Year 3: renewal

gov.il renewal page:

1. From 3 months before expiry a reminder arrives about the renewal refresher. A chart on the page shows when a regular refresher and when a renewal refresher is due.
2. Sign in to the government personal area, open the "כלי ירייה" tab and click "להורדת הטפסים" to get the personal list of documents and the forms.
3. Pay the fee through the government payment service.
4. Go to an authorised range with the payment confirmation and the documents. A photo is taken at the range for the new card. Complete the renewal refresher.
5. After the refresher the range issues a temporary license signed by the range. The license is valid for 3 years.
6. The permanent plastic license is mailed to the address registered with the Population Authority, unless another address was given.

The criteria page adds that the health declaration and undertaking are signed on every renewal. The 2014 procedure lists the renewal notice parts: firearm identification and check, consent form, training confirmation, temporary license, payment slip, health declaration, safe undertaking, and instructions to the holder. It also required a valid criterion at renewal, a home safe meeting the requirements, and no negative opinion from the police, the Health Ministry, welfare or the Nature and Parks Authority.

Active reservists: 50 percent off the license fee, provided the renewal was done by the set date and the eligibility card is shown.

## 4. Missing the refresher: the 72-hour rule

gov.il (both the refresher and renewal pages): a holder who did not complete the refresher on time must deposit the firearm at a licensed dealer within 72 hours of receiving the licensing official's notice of non-completion. A holder who does not deposit it will have the license cancelled and must deposit the firearm immediately at a police station. After cancellation, getting a license again requires a new application, the training program, and meeting the criteria in force at the time of the new application.

## 5. Late renewal: deposit and surcharge

gov.il fee page, section "תוספת תשלום אגרה למי שלא חידש את רישיונו במועד":

- The license must be renewed before expiry. A holder who did not renew on time must deposit the firearm at the Israel Police immediately, because holding a firearm without a valid license is a criminal offence.
- After depositing, if renewal is approved, a surcharge is added to the renewal fee according to the delay:

| Delay after expiry | Surcharge |
|--------------------|-----------|
| Up to 6 months | The fee for one year of that license |
| 6 to 12 months | The fee for three years of that license |
| Over 12 months | For each year of delay or part of it, the fee for four years of that license |

The fee page notes the amounts are shown for convenience and the Firearms (License Fees) Regulations, 5766-2005, prevail.

The 2014 renewal procedure adds internal shape, not binding today: renewal allowed if a valid criterion still applies and less than 6 months passed; between 3 and 6 months the district supervisor's approval is needed; a one-month temporary license may be issued to finish the process; renewal after deposit is possible if less than 3 years passed since expiry; over 3 years without a valid license, a 3-year non-renewal period from the deposit date may be considered.

## 6. Emergency extensions

Do not assume an extension exists; each one is a temporary order with fixed dates.

- 26.10.2023 and 30.01.2024 orders (gov.il news 13.12.2023, updated 18.02.2024): licenses expiring 31.10.2023 to 31.03.2024 were extended by six months each; refresher deadlines in the same months extended by six months; conditional approvals issued June to December 2023 with an active application extended by three months from their original expiry.
- 21.05.2024 (gov.il topic page news): holders whose license expired 31.5.2024 could request a 30-day extension by 26.5.2024 through the individual notice, with no further extensions.
- 23.04.2026 (gov.il news, updated 26.04.2026): the National Security Committee approved a temporary order; refreshers and renewals due 31.3.2026, 30.4.2026 and 31.5.2026 were extended to 30.6.2026, 31.7.2026 and 31.8.2026 respectively. Notices go by email and SMS to the contact details on file; updating them is the holder's responsibility.

## 7. License copy and cards that never arrived

gov.il "הוצאת העתק לרישיון":

- For a valid license that was stolen, lost, worn or never received; private, organisational, instructor and range-supervisor licenses.
- Pay online (fee table, 71 NIS as of 04.01.2026), then apply online or at a self-service kiosk.
- A permanent card is sent within 90 days of the request to the address on file at the department. Until then print the temporary paper license after applying.
- If 90 days passed since the license was produced and it did not arrive, and less than 6 months passed and the address did not change, call the service center to check eligibility for a free card.

## 8. Updating address and contact details

Two registries matter:

1. The department's own contact details, used for reminders, SMS and the card: update through the department's contact-update form at https://auth.govforms.gov.il/mw/forms/form19@mops.gov.il . The gov.il license-copy page links to it under the text "לעדכון פרטי התקשרות באגף לרישוי כלי ירייה" (link read in a browser on 14.09.2026; the form itself sits behind the national identification login).
2. The Population Authority address, used for the plastic card after renewal unless another address was given.

Emergency notices are sent to the email and mobile on file; the 13.12.2023 notice states that keeping them current is the holder's responsibility.
