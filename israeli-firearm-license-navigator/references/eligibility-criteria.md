# Eligibility: Threshold Conditions and Criteria

Sources: gov.il criteria page "תבחינים למתן רישיון להחזקת כלי ירייה פרטי" (published 21.07.2025, updated 30.06.2026) and the Firearms Regulations (Threshold Conditions and Criteria for a Private Firearm License and Additional Provisions), 5784-2023 (תקנות כלי הירייה (תנאי סף ותבחינים לקבלת רישיון פרטי לכלי ירייה והוראות נוספות), התשפ"ד-2023). Read on 14.09.2026.

The gov.il page states that the criteria list is closed and exhaustive: no private license or permit can be issued without meeting one of them.

## 1. Threshold conditions (tnai saf)

All of these must hold at the time of application. The regulation is section 2; the gov.il summary reads:

| # | Condition | Notes |
|---|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Registered with the Interior Ministry as a citizen or permanent resident, and resident in Israel continuously for the 3 years before the application | Exception: a person holding a teudat oleh under the Law of Return may apply under the "place of residence" criterion only, if the police approved their fitness and the other conditions hold |
| 2 | Basic command of Hebrew, enough to be questioned, understand instructions, read procedures and keep the required records | Tested in practice at the interview and the range |
| 3 | A declaration of medical fitness to carry a firearm (hatzharat briut), signed by the applicant and by a physician | The gov.il application page says "family physician". The declaration and undertaking are signed again on every renewal |
| 4 | Completion of the training program under the Firearms (Training) Regulations, 5778-2018 | See `process-stages.md` |
| 5 | A police officer under section 11(2) of the Law found no impediment on grounds of public peace and safety | The police also recommend on the residence and workplace criteria |

The same threshold requirements apply to a person requesting a permit (teudat harsha'a) to hold an organisational firearm under sections 9 to 10c of the Law.

## 2. Age and service rules

From the gov.il criteria page (section 2 of the summary):

| Situation | Minimum age |
|-----------|-------------|
| Completed regular service (sherut sadir) as defined in section 11c(b) of the Law | 18 |
| Completed 2 years of civil service (sherut ezrachi) | 21 |
| For the "place of residence" and "place of work or study" criteria: completed one year of regular service as a combat soldier, or two years of regular service | 21 |
| For the same two criteria: a woman who completed one year of regular service or one year of civil service | 21 |
| Israeli citizen who did not serve as above | 27 |
| Permanent resident who is not a citizen and did not serve as above | 45 |

"Regular service" in section 11c(b)(3) of the Law means service under the Defence Service Law (Consolidated Version), 5746-1986, for the period in section 15 or 16 of that law, as applicable.

## 3. Amendment 25 (2025)

Under Amendment 25 to the Firearms Law, 5785-2025, the following may also apply from age 18, and the licensing official will ask for medical documents about the injury or the medical reason:

- A soldier whom an authorised IDF officer reported to the department as not having completed regular service because of an injury in operational circumstances.
- A soldier whom an authorised IDF officer reported as having served as a combat soldier (lochem) under army orders and discharged for medical reasons.

## 4. The 16 criteria (tavchinim)

The appendix (tosefet) to the 2023 regulations. Thresholds below are as summarised from the regulation text on Wikisource on 14.09.2026; where the summary says "police recommendation", the recommendation is per settlement or workplace and is not published as a list, which is why the official calculator is the only reliable eligibility check.

| # | Criterion | Core condition | Proof |
|---|-----------|----------------|-------|
| 1 | Place of residence (makom megurim) | Centre of life in a settlement the police recommended as justifying a license | Police recommendation via the system; the calculator reflects it |
| 2 | Place of work or study | Works most of the working week, or studies at least two days a week, in a place the police recommended | Police recommendation; employer or institution affidavit (see section 5) |
| 3 | Tour guide (moreh derech) | Valid national tour-guide license from the Ministry of Tourism | The license, plus the affidavit form |
| 4 | Firefighter (kabai) | Firefighter or volunteer with 2 or more years, confirmed by the Fire and Rescue Authority; a former firefighter within 30 days of leaving after 2 or more years; security-role employees of the authority with 2 or more years | Authority confirmation, affidavit form |
| 5 | Rescue-body workers and volunteers | Employee or active volunteer for at least one year in actual rescue activity in a recognised body (Magen David Adom, ZAKA, United Hatzalah and others) | Body confirmation; affidavit forms exist for MDA, ZAKA, United Hatzalah |
| 6 | Recognised farmer (chaklai) | Main occupation in agriculture with an approved need; self-employed farmer, first-degree family member, or agricultural employee | Security officer or farmers' federation recommendation; affidavit forms for each sub-type |
| 7 | Explosives transporter | Valid permit to transport explosives under the Explosives Law | The permit |
| 8 | Security officer or security manager (memuneh bitachon) | As defined in the Regulation of Security in Public Bodies Law, or a security manager appointed on police requirement | Police confirmation |
| 9 | Shooting instructor (madrich yeri) | Active licensed instructor with at least one year of seniority who trained at least 60 trainees in the year before the application | Approved training records |
| 10 | Service in the security forces | (a) IDF officer from rank of lieutenant, or NCO from rank of first sergeant, with 5 or more years of career service or 60 or more reserve days in 6 years; (b) combat soldier in a special unit; (c) IDF discharged with rifleman 07 training or higher; (d) discharged with a combat certificate (teudat lochem); (e) discharged from 1999 and earlier with rifle training; (f) discharged from permanent instructor or commander roles | IDF certificates from the IDF approvals site, affidavit form "service in the security forces" |
| 11 | Israel Police service | (a) officer serving 2 or more years, or 2 or more years of career service; (b) Border Police combatant 2 or more years; (c) volunteer with 15 years, applying within 10 years of ending; (d) active volunteer living in a recommended settlement with 1 to 2 or more years of service; (e) compulsory-service graduate with 18 or more months in patrol, dispatcher or investigator roles, with confirmation | Police confirmation |
| 11a | Prison Service | Warden serving 2 or more years in career service, or a graduate of such a role | Prison Service confirmation |
| 12 | Unique training | Graduates or employees of the Airports Authority guard course, the ISA-approved guard course, a Defence Ministry security course, the Knesset Guard "Oz" unit, or an advanced guard course A or B in a guided body | Course certificate; affidavit form "security guard advanced course A or B" |
| 13 | Athlete, active shooter | Olympic shooting: association member 2 or more years and 4 or more completed competitions each season; practical shooting: member 3 or more years and 4 or more competitions each season; Paralympic: IDF disabled veterans association member 2 or more years and 4 or more competitions | Federation confirmation |
| 14 | Hunter (tzayad) | Valid hunting license or permit under the Wildlife Protection Regulations | The license or permit |
| 15 | Veterinary needs | Licensed veterinarian, or a permanent employee under their supervision | Veterinary license |
| 16 | Pest controller (madbir) | Valid pest-control license under the Sanitary Pest Control Regulation Law | The license |

Under criterion 4 (place of work or study), gov.il adds (section 4 of the criteria page): an applicant who already holds a permit from a special-license holder (an eligible settlement, plant, or government office) that is not limited to a place or time may be refused, and must choose between carrying an organisational firearm and a private license.

## 5. Affidavit forms published by gov.il

The criteria page attaches affidavit (tatzhir) forms for: salaried employee in an eligible place; self-employed in an eligible place; student in an eligible place with institution confirmation; security guard advanced course A or B; ZAKA search, rescue and recovery; ZAKA "moment of truth"; Magen David Adom; United Hatzalah; firefighter; service in the security forces; employee of a farmer; self-employed farmer; member of an agricultural cooperative; first-degree family member of a farmer; certified tour guide; YATAR (rapid response team). Send the user to download the current form from the page rather than describing its fields, because the forms change without notice.

## 6. Limits

Regulation 7(a): for criteria 1 to 12 the private license covers one handgun (ekdach) and ammunition; for criteria 13 to 16 the licensing official sets the type and quantity. No more than four private licenses are issued to one applicant even if several criteria apply.

Regulation 7(b): when a private license is issued, the licensing official sets the storage conditions. In practice this is the home-safe undertaking; see `storage-ammunition-and-incidents.md`.

## 7. When a criterion stops applying

From the criteria page (section 5) and regulation 3(e) to (f):

1. When the licensing official learns that the criterion under which the license was issued no longer holds, they notify the holder in writing.
2. The holder may ask to prove the criterion within 30 days of receiving the notice.
3. If not proven within 30 days, the holder must deposit the firearm and ammunition at a licensed dealer, and may prove another criterion within a period not exceeding 6 months from the first notice.
4. The total time to prove a criterion before the license is cancelled is 6 months from the first notice.

Typical triggers: moving out of a recommended settlement, leaving a recommended workplace, ending volunteer service, or a lapsed professional license.
