# Process Stages: From Application to the First Card

Sources: gov.il service page "בקשת רישיון לכלי ירייה פרטי" (read in a browser on 14.09.2026, including the collapsed sections "how to apply" and "after applying"), gov.il guide "הכשרות במטווח לבעלי רישיון לכלי ירייה פרטי" (published 14.08.2019), gov.il page "רכישת כלי ירייה מאדם פרטי על ידי אדם פרטי", the fee table (updated 04.01.2026), the Knesset Research and Information Center review "היבטים ברישוי כלי ירייה פרטי בישראל" (13.02.2024), and department procedure 12.02.04 (version 1, 1.4.2014, published under freedom of information).

## 1. Stage 1: Eligibility check

Before applying, gov.il recommends the eligibility calculator: https://www.gov.il/apps/mops/firearm_license_calculator/ . It asks about status, service, age, residence and workplace, and reflects the police recommendations per settlement that are not published as a list. Consult `eligibility-criteria.md` for the criteria and the documents each one needs.

## 2. Stage 2: Online application

Where: the form "בקשה לקבלת רישיון כלי ירייה פרטי" at https://auth.govforms.gov.il/mw/forms/WeaponLicense@mops.gov.il , after signing in with the national identification system (ma'arechet ha-hizdahut ha-leumit). Registration is a one-time step; afterwards the personal area opens with a password.

Attach (gov.il list):

- Health declaration signed by a family physician (the form is attached to the page as a PDF).
- Copy of the identity card and its appendix (sefach).
- Confirmation of national or civil service, or an exemption; IDF confirmations are ordered on the IDF approvals site (works with Chrome).
- Additional documents supporting the application data, according to the criterion (see the affidavit list in `eligibility-criteria.md`).

After submission an SMS arrives with the request number and handling times, and progress can be tracked in the government personal area.

## 3. Stage 3: Interview or exemption

gov.il: after the file is checked for completeness, the department examines eligibility for an interview exemption, or sends an SMS with a link to book a personal phone interview.

The Knesset review (13.02.2024) adds that from May 2023 applicants under the security-forces-service criterion and the eligible-residence criterion are exempt from the interview, that the rest get a phone interview, and that the interviewing official may summon a frontal interview when something raises a flag. The Ministry described the call as a clarification of the application data rather than an interview.

During the interview the licensing official can approve the shortened training track (see stage 5). A user who qualifies for it should raise it at this point, with the documents.

## 4. Stage 4: Conditional approval (ishur mutne) and the fee

After the interview, eligibility for a conditional approval is examined. The Knesset review states that the conditional approval is valid for half a year, during which the applicant pays the fee, completes the training under the Firearms (Training) Regulations, 2018, and buys the firearm. gov.il's own service page does not print the validity, so quote the Knesset source and tell the user to read the date on the document itself. In December 2023 the Ministry extended approvals issued June to December 2023 by three months, which shows the period is a policy value that can change.

Fee (gov.il fee table, updated 04.01.2026, based on the Firearms (License Fees) Regulations, 5766-2005 and the 5786-2026 fee notice):

| Item | NIS | Note |
|------|-----|------|
| Private carry license, issue or renewal | 71 | Per year or part of a year; the license runs 3 years |
| Purchase of a firearm by a private person | 71 | Per year or part of a year, for the firearm license |
| Replacement of a firearm | 71 | |
| License copy | 71 | |

Pay through the government payment service (voucher 388) or at the postal bank. The fee is computed per year of validity up to three years (gov.il private-sale page: "עלות רישיון כלי הירייה מחושבת לפי מספר שנות תוקף הרישיון (עד שלוש שנים)"), so a 3-year license is three times the annual fee; the voucher attached to the conditional approval shows the amount. The gov.il application page states that reservists get 50 percent off the license fee on presenting the eligibility card; the renewal page adds that on renewal the discount requires renewing by the set date.

The range charges its own price for the training. gov.il does not publish it; the user should ask the range when booking.

## 5. Stage 5: Range training

Go to an authorised range (mitvach mursheh, list on gov.il) with the payment confirmation, a copy of the health declaration and the conditional approval, and train on a firearm identical to the one you intend to buy. The purchase later must match the training in type and caliber.

Regular training (hachshara regila), gov.il:

- 4.5 consecutive hours.
- Theory part, then an exam, pass mark 70 percent. Failing the theory exam blocks the practical part.
- Practical part: 80 rounds of practice, then a 20-round qualification, pass mark 70 percent.
- A candidate who fails may take additional training.
- The theory pass is valid for 3 months from the training date.

Shortened training (hachshara mekutzeret), approved by the licensing official at the interview, for one of:

- Service in a security body, with a certificate of prior knowledge and training (form attached to the gov.il page).
- Training at an authorised range in the 3 years before the application, reported to the authority; if not reported, ask the range for a certificate (form attached).

Content: 2 consecutive hours, theory with exam at 70 percent, 20 practice rounds and a 10-round qualification at 70 percent. Failing sends the candidate to the 4.5-hour program or a shorter one, provided all subjects and both exams are passed.

The Firearms (Training) Regulations, 5778-2018, in force from 12.08.2018, are the legal basis; the detailed program is held at the ranges.

## 6. Stage 6: Purchase

From a dealer (beit mischar, list on gov.il): the dealer performs the ownership transfer, hands over the firearm and ammunition, and issues the paper license. The bill of sale from the dealer states the ammunition quantity.

From a private person (gov.il page "רכישת כלי ירייה מאדם פרטי על ידי אדם פרטי"):

- The buyer brings the conditional approval, the fee payment confirmation, and confirmation of training at an authorised range on the purchased firearm, same type and same caliber.
- The seller brings a valid license card.
- Since the Iron Swords war the bill of sale (shtar mecher) is sent by email to Tservice@mops.gov.il, without visiting a licensing official, provided the seller and buyer identified themselves before a lawyer.
- The bill of sale must state the ammunition quantity transferred; if the seller has none, the buyer attaches a bill of sale from a dealer for the ammunition.
- If the seller's firearm is deposited at the police, the seller should check with the licensing bureau first; a debt may exist on a firearm deposited after expiry.
- The license cost is computed per year of validity, up to three years.

## 7. Stage 7: Safe, paper license, magnetic card

gov.il: a firearm is dangerous; install a home safe and store the firearm in it, per the guide "הנחיות להתקנת כספת ביתית" (see `storage-ammunition-and-incidents.md`). The 2014 procedure had the applicant sign a safe undertaking before the license was produced, and the renewal notice still includes a safe undertaking.

The paper license from the dealer or the range is a temporary license and serves as authority to carry and hold the firearm until the plastic card arrives. gov.il: within 90 days of the ownership transfer the magnetic license is sent to the postal address on file at the department.

If 90 days passed since issue and the card did not arrive (and less than 6 months passed and the address did not change), the service center can check eligibility for a free card; otherwise a copy costs the fee. See `renewal-and-refresher.md`.

## 8. Timelines

Published on gov.il:

- SMS with handling times after submission (the times are shown to the applicant, not on the public page).
- Theory pass valid 3 months.
- Magnetic card within 90 days of transfer.

From the 2014 procedure 12.02.04, useful as a shape of the internal targets but not binding today: a refusal for missing threshold or criterion is sent within 30 days of submission; a reminder for missing documents after 30 days; approval within 30 days of the complete file; an interim letter when the police or the Health Ministry delay, with a further 45 days expected; the magnetic license within about two months of the temporary one.

From the Knesset review: the conditional approval runs half a year, and the whole chain ends with a temporary license followed by a magnetic license valid for 3 years.
