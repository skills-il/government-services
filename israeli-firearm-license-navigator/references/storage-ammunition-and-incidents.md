## 1. Home safe specification

gov.il requirements for a private firearm at home:

- The firearm and ammunition (handgun, magazines, rounds) are stored in a safe locked by a mechanical (key) mechanism. A digital code or other identification is allowed only if the safe can also be opened mechanically with a key.
- Minimum external size: height 25 cm, width 35 cm, depth 25 cm, and large enough for the firearm with mounted accessories, the number of firearms, and the ammunition.
- Body: steel at least 4 mm thick with reinforcement around the frame; a front step that prevents pushing the door inward and acts as a fire step; massive hinges welded to door and frame, internal hinges preferred; door of two 4 mm steel plates welded together (8 mm total).
- Locking: at least 4 bolts, 2 fixed on the hinge side and 2 moving on the opposite side driven by an external handle; a double-bit safe lock ("Kromer", "Kaba", "Mauer" or equivalent) that locks the bolt system.
- Anchoring: at least 4 anchors cast in concrete or massive bolts of at least 14 mm tightened from inside, in at least two directions (not all to one wall), to a wall or floor that resists removal (concrete, steel, concreted block, concreted floor).
- Confirm with the dealer that the safe sold is suited to firearm storage and meets the guideline.

## 2. Storage rules and prohibitions

- Keep the firearm locked and out of reach of everyone else in the home, including adults, children and guests.
- Nobody else, including spouse, household members and especially minors, may have access to the key, code or other opening means. Handing them over is strictly forbidden.
- Do not store another person's firearm in your safe. Several firearms of the same holder may share a safe suited to them under the holder's exclusive control.
- Install the safe in a concealed place not accessible to household members and others.
- A handgun is stored in the safe with the magazine in the magazine well. Do not unload without need; unload only when there is a concern a round is chambered and it poses a safety risk, following the standard presentation on the site.
- Never leave a firearm or ammunition in a vehicle.
- Never store the firearm in a hotel safe; it is not under the holder's control.

## 3. Ammunition quotas and top-ups

gov.il guidance page (published 17.06.2020):

| License type | Quota | Top-up route |
|--------------|-------|--------------|
| Private handgun license | 50 rounds per the license conditions | One-time top-up: sign an undertaking before the dealer, send the dealer's bill of sale for the ammunition and the undertaking form to tservice@mops.gov.il with ID and contact details |
| Hunting criterion, hunting rifle | 700 rounds across all the holder's licenses; top-up once a year | Valid hunter license from the Nature and Parks Authority, consumption or top-up undertaking, dealer bill of sale, submitted to the licensing official |
| Athlete, active shooter | 1,000 rounds per firearm | Recommendation of the shooting federation director, consumption report signed by an instructor or range manager, dealer bill of sale; the official then issues an ammunition license |

A dealer issues a bill of sale for the quantity actually bought at that time; ammunition cannot be supplied in instalments against one bill.

Caution for agents: press reports from late 2023 describe a ministerial decision to raise the private handgun quota from 50 to 100 rounds, including for existing holders. The gov.il guidance page still shows 50 and carries a 2020 publication date. Tell the user the quota that binds them is the one printed on their license conditions, and to confirm with the dealer before buying.

## 4. Loss or theft

Law: section 15(a) of the Firearms Law requires a person whose firearm was lost, destroyed or stolen to notify a police station as early as possible and no later than 48 hours from learning of it. Proving a report within 48 hours is a defence to the criminal charge under that subsection.

Department procedure 12.02.32 (2014):

1. The holder reports to the police station within 48 hours and, in parallel, to the licensing official within 72 hours.
2. The police firearms officer sends a report to the licensing official, who updates the file: holder, firearm details, nature of the event, whether the firearm was seized, and whether the police intend to recommend cancellation.
3. Loss or theft leads to cancellation of the license. The official cancels it, sends a registered letter, and demands the license card back.
4. A license for a replacement firearm requires a new application, judged as new, and the applicant must prove a currently valid criterion; a "preserving criterion" (tavchin meshamer, a grandfathered basis) does not qualify.

Practical checklist: police report with reference number, a copy to the department by email with ID number, the license card ready to return, and a fresh application if a replacement is wanted.

## 5. Selling, replacing, or adding a firearm

Selling to a private person (gov.il): the seller brings a valid license card; the buyer brings a conditional approval, fee payment confirmation, and training confirmation on the same type and caliber; the bill of sale states the ammunition transferred; since the Iron Swords war the bill of sale goes by email to Tservice@mops.gov.il after both parties identified before a lawyer. If the firearm is deposited at the police, the seller should first check for a debt with the licensing bureau.

Replacing a firearm (gov.il "החלפת כלי ירייה"): the page provides a replacement request form and a private bill of sale form, and describes two routes, replacement with a private person and replacement with a dealer. The fee table lists "replacement of a firearm" at 71 NIS (04.01.2026). Training must cover the new firearm's type and caliber.

Adding a firearm: regulation 7(a) limits criteria 1 to 12 to one handgun per private license and sets a ceiling of four private licenses per person; extra firearms need their own basis.

Memento (mazkeret): gov.il has a separate service for holding a firearm as a memento in cases of inheritance or preservation of a personal firearm; it is a different license and outside this skill.

## 6. When the criterion stops applying

From the criteria page: written notice from the licensing official; 30 days to prove the criterion; if not, deposit the firearm and ammunition at a licensed dealer and prove another criterion within up to 6 months of the first notice, after which the license is cancelled. See `eligibility-criteria.md`, section 7.

Every source describes a notice initiated by the official ("נודע לפקיד הרישוי"); none states a duty on the holder to report a move or a job change on their own initiative. Advising early contact with the department is practical advice from this skill and should be labelled as such. Procedure 12.02.08 lists the expiry of the basis (for example a volunteer who stopped, a firefighter who left, a shooter who left the federation) among the grounds for cancellation.

## 7. Absence from home and travel

gov.il storage rules: when the holder is away from home for more than 48 hours and no adult household member is present during that period, the firearm must be deposited for safekeeping with a licensed dealer. Taking a firearm abroad needs an export permit (gov.il service "ייצוא כלי ירייה (אישי)"), outside this skill.
