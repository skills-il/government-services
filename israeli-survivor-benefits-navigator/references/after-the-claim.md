# After the Claim: Staying Abroad and Income Tax

Rules that matter once the survivor's allowance is being paid. Verified 2026-09-25.

## Staying abroad (Bituach Leumi survivor's allowance)

Source: https://www.btl.gov.il/benefits/Survivors_%20Insurance/Pages/%d7%a9%d7%94%d7%99%d7%99%d7%94%20%d7%91%d7%97%d7%95%d7%9c.aspx

- A survivor staying abroad is paid the allowance for up to 3 months, into the Israeli bank account.
- Anyone intending to stay abroad for more than 3 months must notify the senior-citizen and survivors department of the handling branch. Continued payment beyond that period is possible only under special conditions, so the family should ask before leaving rather than after the payments stop.
- A survivor living abroad who receives the allowance must send Bituach Leumi a signed "life certificate" (אישור חיים) once a year, signed by a consulate, a recognised notary, a local authority or a social-welfare body.
- The income supplement (tosefet hashlamat hachnasa) is stricter. A recipient below retirement age must report every trip abroad, and is not paid the supplement for any full calendar month spent abroad. From the second trip in the same calendar year, the supplement is also withheld for the month of departure and the month of return.
- The special survivors' allowance (form 4506) is stricter still: on a temporary stay it continues for only one further calendar month once a month has passed from departure, and on a long stay it stops from the month of departure.

## Income tax on survivors' payments

Source: Income Tax Ordinance, section 9, https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/255_001.htm

| Payment | Tax treatment |
|---|---|
| Bituach Leumi survivor's allowance (chapter 11 of the National Insurance Law) | Exempt, section 9(6ג) |
| Bituach Leumi dependents' allowance for a work-related death | Exempt, section 9(6ג) |
| Survivors' pension derived from exempt payments | Exempt, section 9(6ו)(א) |
| Survivors' pension from a pension fund, an insurance plan or a budgetary pension, not derived from exempt payments | Exempt only up to the qualifying-pension ceiling (תקרת הקצבה המזכה), section 9(6ו)(ב); the excess is taxable |

Do not tell a family that its whole pension-fund survivors' pension is tax-free: unless it derives from exempt payments, it is exempt only up to the ceiling. Whether a given pension derives from exempt payments is for the fund and a tax advisor to confirm. For the current ceiling and how it interacts with other pensions the survivor draws, refer them to a tax advisor or to the Israel Tax Authority.
