# The Department's own programmes (besides the Pikadon, the grant and Mimadim)

Verified 2026-10-18 against the hachvana pages listed under Sources. Scholarships 44, 45, 46, the
technician scholarship and mechina funding are paid by the Department's Fund, outside the Pikadon,
so they belong in this skill and are NOT third-party scholarships. The "משפיעים בלימודים"
living-cost scholarship is a donation of the Edmond de Rothschild Foundation and the Association
for the Wellbeing of Israel's Soldiers, run with the Department and paid through the Association.
Until v1.6.0 the skill routed all of them to `israeli-academic-scholarships`.

## Mechina and bagrut completion: the Fund pays first

The page is explicit: "הסיוע אינו מוענק על חשבון הפיקדון", and "מי שלא יימצא זכאי לסיוע במימון
מלא או חלקי של הלימודים במכינה, יוכל לממן את שכר הלימוד על חשבון הפיקדון האישי". So the Pikadon
pays a mechina only for someone the Fund turns down. Tuition and living allowance require 80%
attendance. If the 5-year window ends mid-course, funding continues to the end of the mechina.

| Population | Window at registration | Funding | How to register |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lone soldiers | Up to 10 years | Full tuition + living allowance, automatic | Via the mechina (discharged from July 2021: IDF digital-wallet lone-soldier confirmation) |
| Combat veterans with gold-honour certificate | Up to 5 years | Full tuition automatic; living allowance by socio-economic criteria | Via the mechina office with the digital-wallet gold-honour confirmation |
| Olim | Up to 7 years in Israel | Full tuition + living allowance | Via the mechina, with the oleh certificate |
| Ethiopian-Israeli dischargees | Up to 5 years | Full tuition + living allowance | Via the mechina, with ID cards |
| Minorities, Haredi IDF dischargees, tashmash recipients | Up to 5 years | Full tuition + living allowance | Ask the district office or *5266 for form no. 2, then register with the mechina |
| Active reservists meeting socio-economic criteria | Up to 10 years | By criteria | Form no. 2, then the mechina |
| Every dischargee and SLE completer | Up to 5 years | Partial to full, by socio-economic criteria | Via the mechina office |

Source: https://www.hachvana.mod.gov.il/MainEducation/TwelveYearsStudies/Pages/PreAcademic.aspx

## Combat veterans (gold-honour certificate), including Swords of Iron

From https://www.hachvana.mod.gov.il/ExtraBenefits/Pages/combat.aspx:

- **Tochnit Amit**: the MoD, recognising "תרומתכם במלחמת חרבות ברזל", offers through an app "בחינם
  לגמרי" therapy, training, sport, workshops and mental-health care.
- **Mechina**: full tuition, plus a monthly living allowance subject to socio-economic criteria.
- **Vocational courses**: a catalogue of courses in which combat veterans get "מימון כמעט מלא".
  Check it before spending the Pikadon (purpose #2) on a course.
- **Lochamim LeHitech**: with the IDF and Atidim, courses in software development, QA and cyber.
- **Mimadim**: 100% for a first or second degree, or an arts certificate.

No war-specific Pikadon or grant top-up for discharged conscripts was found as of 2026-10-18. The
reservist grants are a different population (`israeli-miluim-manager`).

## Wounded and bereaved

The Department lists discharged IDF wounded (פצועי צה"ל) and orphans (יתומי מערכות ישראל) as
populations with their own pages. Recognition of an injury or mental-health condition from service
is a claim to the MoD Rehabilitation Division (אגף השיקום, https://shikum.mod.gov.il/), outside this skill. A soldier discharged for an injury in training or operations, as determined by the Head of Manpower, is deemed to have completed full compulsory service for the Pikadon and grant (s.10A(b)). No filing deadline
is stated here because none was verified against the statute; tell the user to file early.

Hub: https://www.hachvana.mod.gov.il/MainEducation/HachvanaScholarship/Pages/default.aspx

All figures are for academic year תשפ"ו (2025-2026). The תשפ"ז cycle had not been published on
2026-10-18; re-read each page before quoting a deadline.

## Stacking rules (verbatim where it matters)

- **44, 45 and 46 do not stack with each other**: "ניתן לממש רק אחת מהטבות הפריפריה של האגף."
- **46 does not stack with Mimadim**: "שילוב עם מלגות "פריפריה" אחרות (44, 45) או "ממדים ללימודים": אין כפל מלגות."
- **44 and Mimadim**: a first-year student eligible for both takes 44 first and Mimadim "החל משנת הלימודים השנייה בלבד".
- **45 and Mimadim combine**: 85% to the bank account and the remaining 15% to the Pikadon account.

## Windows

44, 45 and 46 run from 3 months before discharge to 5 years after the end of regular service, and
up to 10 years for lone soldiers and active reservists (the s.19(a1)(1) extension for s.7(a)
benefits; see SKILL.md Step 5). The technician page states 5 and 10 years without the 3-month
lead.
