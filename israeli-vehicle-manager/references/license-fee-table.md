# Annual Licence Fee Table (אגרת רישוי) - effective 1 April 2026

Source: Ministry of Transport fee board, <https://www.gov.il/he/pages/drivers-car-license-fee-boards> (page last updated 18.06.2026). Amounts in NIS. The board is re-linked to the CPI every April, so treat the shekel amounts as a snapshot and re-check the board before quoting a number to a user. **The lookup structure below is stable; only the amounts move.**

## How to find your row (this is the part that does not change)

1. **Find your licence group (קבוצת רישוי), 1 to 7.** It is printed on the **left-hand side of your vehicle licence (רישיון רכב)**. The group is set by the model's consumer price when the model was first registered, not by what you paid for the car.
2. **Pick the column** matching the vehicle's year of first road registration / year of manufacture.
3. **Add the Israeli Public Broadcasting Corporation levy of 139 NIS** on top of the table amount. (The board's general fee table separately lists a 135 NIS line for the same item. The amount shown on your renewal invoice is the one you pay.)

Note on the source: the board renders the last column header defectively (it displays as "106 ומטה"). Given the sequence of the preceding columns (2024-2026, 2021-2023, 2017-2020) the band is 2016 and older.

## Payment channels

Payment can be split into up to 12 instalments. Channels listed on the board: the government payment service online, self-service terminals (the new licence is issued on the spot), the telephone centre 4515*, and cash at Israel Post bank branches only. When paying online or at the post office, the amount in the computerised system governs, not the amount printed on the form you are holding.
