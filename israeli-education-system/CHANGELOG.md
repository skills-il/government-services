# Changelog

## 1.5.0 - 2026-08-27

Two facts in this skill were wrong in ways that cost a student money or a year, and both had passed two prior review cycles.

**The psychometric fee.** The previous cycle "corrected" it from 665 to 560; 560 was wrong, and its evidence entry cited a PDF that returns 404 with the string `404 Not Found` sitting in the snippet field, so the gate was inert on it. Reading the live NITE table row by row shows something more careful than a simple reversal: **665 is the price cell of the 2-3.9.2026 sitting**, whose registration closed on 8.7.2026, and NITE publishes **no price at all** for the December 2026, April 2027 or July 2027 sittings. The skill now tells the agent to read the price off the row for the sitting the student is actually taking, and says so explicitly rather than quoting a single number.

**The maths Bagrut bonus.** The skill asserted +35 three times, hardcoded it in the calculator, and carried a Gotcha warning users against the correct figure. Its only source was a commercial prep company. The Technion, the one Israeli university publishing a complete readable bonus table, publishes **+30**. Tel Aviv and the Hebrew University expose only JavaScript calculators, so no universal figure is defensible at all. The skill now quotes the Technion table as the Technion's, adds the three rules that travel with it (the grade-60 condition, the mitzraf cluster that levels maths and two qualifying sciences at +30 each rather than keeping maths highest, and the double-weighting of maths UNITS that moves a Technion sekhem more than any bonus), and adds the 4-unit tier that both the prose and the script had omitted entirely, silently scoring 4-unit students at zero.

**Timing, which the skill had none of.** It is late August 2026 and the file was written as a retrospective: it described the 14.10.2026 December registration deadline in the past tense as closed when it is still open, and treated a sitting six days away as historic. It also never mapped a sitting to an admission year, though the December PET reports on 20.1.2027 and therefore cannot serve a תשפ"ז application at all. A new Step 9 adds the registration-deadline table, the sitting-to-year rule, and the fact that institutions have their own registration windows with medicine and architecture closing early and offering no late option.

**Other corrections, each verified against a primary page.** Secondary schools and grades 13-14 end on **20 June**, not the 19 June the skill had elevated to a named gotcha; kindergartens and elementary end 30 June; the year opens 1.9.2026; and the vacation calendar is published per sector rather than nationally. Amir and Amiram are no longer bookable at NITE, Amirnet is the English placement test at 315 NIS with a 35-day minimum between sittings, and Amirnet **already exists** rather than starting in December 2026. The December PET covers two domains across five multiple-choice sections plus a writing task, an hour shorter, not "2 sections". September and December sittings are Hebrew and Arabic only, and Spanish is not offered at all. Medicine is screened by **three** systems (MOR, MIRKAM, MERAV), NITE **summons candidates from university-supplied lists** rather than taking self-registrations, and from 2026 **no overall score is computed**: components are reported separately and each institution weights them itself. `edu.gov.il/special/iron-swords` is dead and `edu.gov.il` itself now redirects to a generic landing page; the live parent-facing surface is `parents.education.gov.il`.

**Numbers that could not be sourced are now labelled, not quietly asserted.** The Iron Swords Bagrut percentages traced to a local news site and the ministry's own תשפ"ז material does not mention the accommodations at all, so the skill states the higher-education route (extended into תשפ"ז as a per-institution appeals track with day-count thresholds) and refuses to quote a Bagrut percentage. The score-to-percentile table had no source and contradicted the calculator's own bands, so both now describe standing relative to the published mean instead of a rank. The sekhem weightings and admission thresholds are labelled unsourced in place. The olim psychometric exemption, the one point where the skill told a student to skip a paid national exam, is now per-institution and notes that a PET waiver does not waive YAEL. The Bagrut pass mark is left hedged rather than flipped, because neither 55 nor 56 could be confirmed this cycle and flipping it on an unverified assertion is how the fee error happened in the first place.

**Script.** The bonus table, the cluster rule, the grade-60 gate and the 4-unit tier are all modelled and hand-checked. The `sekhem` subcommand no longer prints per-program eligibility verdicts: it was emitting `[OK] Medicine` from an invented rescaling and unsourced thresholds, contradicting the skill's own warning that a medicine candidate outside the selection process is out of the cycle. It now prints the components with an explicit statement that the formula is not any university's. The retake "no cap" line, the exact percentile ranks, the Spanish language claim and the 21-unit Yes/No verdict were all corrected or hedged to match the prose.
