## English Placement Tests (Distinct from Bagrut English)
| Test | Hebrew | Purpose |
|------|--------|---------|
| Amirnet | אמירנט | The English placement test. Computerized, 315 NIS, 35-day minimum between sittings. NITE no longer lists Amir (אמי"ר) or Amiram (אמיר"ם); if a user names either, they mean Amirnet |
| Yael | יע"ל | Hebrew proficiency for non-Hebrew speakers (olim, internationals) |
| PET English removal | | From the December 2026 sitting the PET drops English; Amirnet, which already exists, takes over. Admissions impact starts from 5788 (October 2027) |
| MOR / MIRKAM / MERAV | מו"ר / מרק"ם / מר"ב | The THREE NITE selection systems for medicine, not one. From 2026 no overall score is computed: each component is reported separately and each institution weights them itself. NITE summons candidates from university-supplied lists rather than taking self-registrations |

## Technion Bagrut bonus table (moved from SKILL.md Step 3)

Transcribed from admissions.technion.ac.il/calculation-of-the-median-grade, read 2026-08-27. The Technion is used because it is the only Israeli university publishing a complete readable table; Tel Aviv and the Hebrew University expose only JavaScript calculators.

| Level | Subjects | Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| 5 units | Mathematics | **+30** |
| 5 units | Physics, Chemistry, Biology, technological subjects, and recognized humanities and languages (literature, Tanach, history, **English**, Arabic, Hebrew language for Arabic speakers) | **+25** |
| 5 units | Any other listed subject | **+20** |
| 4 units | The Technion publishes a 4-unit value of **+10** for the "additional subjects" tables and for a gemer project. It does NOT publish a 4-unit bonus for maths, the sciences, English or the recognized-languages tier: that row of its main table is 5-unit only. Treat +10 as the floor for listed additional subjects and verify anything else on the program calculator. |
| Gemer project | 5 units +20, 4 units +10, only if the subject name is on the bonus list | |

Three rules that travel with the table and that summaries routinely drop:

- **The bonus applies only from a grade of 60 and up**, and it is additive: a 90 in maths becomes 120.
- **The cluster bonus (mitzraf).** 5-unit maths PLUS either two 5-unit sciences (biology, chemistry, physics) or one science and one technological subject (computer science, machine control, electronics and computers, engineering sciences, biotechnology and its variants) raises **each of those subjects, maths included, to +30**. Maths does not stay higher than the sciences; the whole cluster levels at 30. A gemer project never qualifies.
- **The Technion also double-weights maths UNITS in the average** for every track except architecture and landscape architecture: 5 units counts as 10, 4 counts as 8. On a Technion sekhem this moves the number more than the bonus does, and no other formula in this skill has an equivalent.
