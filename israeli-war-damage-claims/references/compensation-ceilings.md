# Compensation Ceilings for Home Contents (War Damage)

Legal basis: תקנות מס רכוש וקרן פיצויים (תשלום פיצויים) (חפצים ביתיים), תשל"ג-1973,
under חוק מס רכוש וקרן פיצויים, תשכ"א-1961.
Administered by: Israel Tax Authority (רשות המסים בישראל), Compensation Fund (קרן הפיצויים).

## Which column applies

The category amounts are index-linked and updated on 1 January and 1 July (regulation 7(ב)). Each
column is published as applying "מיום" (from) its date. No source says whether the damage
date or the claim date selects the column:

- Damage and claim in the same half-year: use that column
- Damage and claim on either side of 1 January or 1 July (for example Shaagat HaAri damage
  in March 2026, claimed after 1 July 2026): show both columns and ask the Fund which applies
- The worksheet shows ceilings and the amount above them; it never states what the Fund will pay

The newer column is not uniformly higher. Every furniture and electrical cell went DOWN
between the two columns; clothing and other household items went up.

## How a household ceiling is built

Base column (single, or couple / single parent) + one child column per child under 18 +
one adult column per additional adult living in the home, computed **per category**.

- The couple column is for a married couple ("לזוג נשוי") or a single parent. A single parent
  is "הורה שאינו חי עם בן זוג, ושיש לו ילדים החיים עמו שבשנת המס טרם מלאו להם שמונה עשרה שנים".
- A parent whose children are all 18 or over is NOT a single parent for this purpose: single
  column plus one adult column per adult child (for example 51,436 + 18,113 = 69,549 from
  01.07.2026, not 84,007 + 18,113).
- Goods of spouses and of household members living with them are treated as one owner's goods
  (definition of "בעל"). Unrelated co-residents, a second family in the same flat, and an
  unmarried couple: confirm with the Fund how they are counted.
- List only items the household owns and uses personally (regulation 2, "לשימושו האישי").
  Leave out items the household does not own, such as a landlord's furniture. For an item
  used partly for work, ask the appraiser whether it is a household item or business
  equipment.

The four ceilings are **per category and not interchangeable** (regulation 2: the
compensation for all household items "מאותו הסוג" may not exceed the amount set for that
type). Unused furniture headroom does not top up an over-ceiling electrical loss.

## How items are valued

Regulation 2 pays "בגובה הוצאות שיקום הנזק", and "שיקום הנזק" is defined as returning the
item to its pre-damage state or, if that is not possible, "רכישתו של חפץ ביתי דומה חדש
במקומו". In practice: repair cost, or the price of a similar new item, with no deduction
for age.

## What counts as a household item (חפץ ביתי)

Verbatim from the regulation's definition: "רהיטים, ביגוד, כלים, מכשירים לשימוש
ביתי, ספרים ותקליטים, המצויים בבית מגוריו של אדם ואינם נכס כהגדרתו בחוק, למעט
תכשיטים, חפצי אמנות ועתיקות".

"Other household items" is the residual category: anything that is not furniture, clothing
or an electrical appliance (a pot, a towel, a book, a toy). It is the category claimants most
often leave unused. Ask the appraiser about borderline items.

## What is NOT covered

- Jewellery (תכשיטים)
- Artworks (חפצי אמנות) and antiques (עתיקות)
- Cash and cheques

## Business property (not household contents)

- Equipment: market value of the equipment in its pre-damage condition
- Stock: cost of the stock excluding VAT
- Attach purchase invoices for the stock and Form 11 (טופס י"א) for the equipment

## Structure and vehicles

Not settled from the contents table. Fund staff, with engineers and appraisers, assess
the building; vehicles are assessed by the Fund's appraiser, who must approve a repair
before it is done. Both are paid on "הנזק הממשי" (regulation 1 of the war-damage
regulations): the drop in value or the restoration cost, whichever is lower, plus the cost
of reducing the damage. The Fund pays only up to the actual damage, net of insurer or third-party payments
(regulation 3); property tax owed is deducted unless the director decides otherwise
(regulation 4). A written-off vehicle gets the drop in value (pre-damage value minus the
wreck's value), not the repair cost.

## Optional extended insurance (ביטוח רשות של חפצים ביתיים)

For contents worth more than the ceilings above:
- Declared per category; for a declared category the insurance replaces the free cover
  (regulation 4(ב)), and it covers only damage after the declaration and the full year's
  premium (regulation 4(א))
- Premium: 0.3% per year of the additional value insured above the ceiling (regulation 3(ב))
- Maximum additional value: NIS 1,049,113 per the Tax Authority guide updated 01.07.2026.
  Kol Zchut still shows NIS 1,037,100 "נכון ל-2026", the earlier figure.
- Purchased online, or on a declaration form at a Tax Authority office
- Takes effect after the premium is paid and the declaration is accepted; valid to 31.12
  of that year, so it must be in place before the damage and renewed every year
- Keep the declaration; the Fund may check the declared items exist
