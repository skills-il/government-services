# Property Damage Claims Process Flowchart

## Key Deadlines

| Deadline | Timeframe | From When | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notice of damage | Two weeks | Date of damage | Regulation 5(א); Tax Authority guide |
| Claim for compensation | Three months per the Tax Authority guide; one month in the base regulation text | Date of damage | Tax Authority guide; regulation 5(ב) |
| Extension of either | Discretionary, for a reasonable cause | Ask before the deadline | Regulation 5(ג) |
| Director's decision | Within six months | Date the claim was filed | Regulation 8(ב) |
| Payment | Within 30 days | Date of the decision | Regulation 8(ג) |
| Fast-track invoice | 30 days | Date of the fast-track payment | Tax Authority claim page |
| Appeal to the ועדת ערר | 30 days | Delivery of the decision | Regulation 11 |
| Mas Rechush response to an appeal | 60 days | Receipt of the appeal | Tax Authority appeal service |
| District Court appeal (question of law only) | 30 days | Receipt of the committee's decision | Regulation 12א |
| Director's correction of a decision | Four years | Date of the decision | Regulation 9(א) |

## Before anything: do not repair or clear

Do not clear equipment, stock or debris, and do not repair, before the Fund's appraiser
has seen the damage. Cracked glass may be cleared first. Vehicles: repair at any garage
only after the Fund's appraiser approves the repair.

## Process: Fast Track (מסלול מהיר)

Available only while the Tax Authority has it open for the event (for Shaagat HaAri, until
28.04.2026). Up to NIS 30,000, structure, household contents, and business equipment and
stock only (no vehicles), and only if no appraiser has visited.

```
1. Damage occurs
   |
2. Confirm the fast track is open for this event and no appraiser has visited
   |
3. Check every damaged item is included (one claim per family per event,
   filed INSTEAD of a regular claim; no later regular claim on the same event.
   A damaged car is outside the fast track: confirm on *4954 first that the
   vehicle claim stays open)
   |
4. Open the online claim, identify, enter the amount and attach matching quotes
   |
5. Receive the SMS link; photograph at the damage site and upload in real time
   (the phone's location is checked)
   |
6. Decision within 7 business days
   |
   +--> Approved: payment; if only a quote was attached, invoice within 30 days
   |    or the payment is clawed back
   +--> Not approved: passed to the regular track, an appraiser assesses
   +--> Partly approved: NO appeal is available on the fast track
```

## Process: Regular Track (מסלול רגיל)

```
1. Damage occurs
   |
2. Notice within two weeks (*4954 or the online claim)
   |
3. Claim within three months (plan for one month), with photos and quotes
   |
4. Appraiser visit (structure) or Fund appraiser approval (vehicle)
   |
5. Director's decision on entitlement, amount and restoration (target six months)
   |
6. Structure: choose self-restoration or a restoration company working for the Fund
   |
7. Payment within 30 days of the decision; may be conditioned on restoration.
   Before the amount is fixed, ask for an advance of up to 50% (regulation 13);
   an overpayment is repaid within 90 days of a demand (regulation 9(ג))
   |
   +--> If the decision is wrong or low: appeal (below)
```

## Process: Appeal (ערר)

```
1. Receive the final decision (or a decision on an extension request)
   |
2. Compute 30 days from delivery; the letter states the deadline
   |
3. Warn: the committee may reduce the award as well as raise it
   |
4. Check the user's own appeal will contain: name and ID, phone and email,
   claim number, amount claimed, grounds in paragraphs, the decision, all
   evidence. Give the grounds as points; the user writes and signs the appeal
   |
5. Send to Ararim@taxes.gov.il or fax 076-8090001 (free; the form is optional)
   |
6. Mas Rechush response within 60 days
   |
7. Hearing before a three-member ועדת ערר; summons by email.
   Not attending can get the appeal struck out
   |
8. Decision
   |
   +--> Error of law: District Court appeal within 30 days
```

No decision for an unreasonable time: write to Mas Rechush through the personal area or
*4954; if there is still no decision, approach the ועדת ערר with the full facts.

## Who Can File

| Person | Can File For |
|---|---|
| Property owner | Structure, and contents they own |
| Whoever funds the restoration | Structure (even if not the owner) |
| Tenant | Contents they own |
| Business owner | Business equipment and stock |
| Vehicle owner (or holder with a power of attorney) | Vehicle, with a police confirmation |
