# Domain Checklist: Israeli War Damage Claims (direct damage)

Scope: direct physical damage from war or hostile acts, compensated by the Tax Authority's
Compensation Fund (קרן הפיצויים, מס רכוש). Built 2026-11-03 from the war-damage
regulations (nevo 273_020), the household-goods regulations (nevo 273_034), the Tax
Authority guide and claim and appeal pages on gov.il, and Kol Zchut.
