# Dmei Avtala Eligibility Rules (2026)

Complete eligibility rule table for Israeli unemployment benefits under the National Insurance Law. Source: Bituach Leumi and Kolzchut.

> All ₪ figures and procedural windows in this document are 2026 values, linked to inflation. They re-link in January 2027. Always verify against btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/zakaut.aspx for the current year before relying on them.

## Core Requirements (All Must Be True)

1. **Israeli resident** at the time of unemployment
2. **Age 20 to 67.** Over 67: claim kitzvat zikna instead. Under 20 is NOT an automatic bar, see the under-20 exception list below
3. **Qualifying period met** (tkufat akhshara): at least 12 months of salaried work (shakhir) out of the 18 months preceding registration. Reduced to **6 months out of 18** only when the user was on chal"t inside the Shaagat HaAri 2026 defining period (28 Feb 2026 to 14 Apr 2026, extendable by ministerial order to 14 May 2026). **That window is CLOSED: for any leave or job loss beginning after 14.5.2026 the 12-month rule is the only rule.** A further reduction to 3 months out of 18 applied to special populations inside the same window
4. **Registered as unemployed** at Sherut HaTaasuka (taasuka.gov.il), with a follow-up in-person branch visit within 14 days of online registration
5. **Actively seeking work**, attending reporting sessions, and accepting suitable offers

## Qualifying Period (tkufat akhshara) Rules

- **Standard track:** 12 months required out of the 18-month lookback window
- **Shaagat HaAri 2026 chal"t track [CLOSED 14.5.2026]:** 6 months required out of 18, and 3 months out of 18 for special populations (operation evacuees; people on a BL or MoD disability allowance; spouses of reservists with 90+ days in the preceding year; MoD-recognised hostile-action casualties and disabled IDF veterans since 7 Oct and their spouses; soldiers discharged within the last year), each with its own required attachment. Applies only if the chal"t fell within the defining period above
- **Lookback window anchor:** starts from the last day of work if the person registers within 3 months, otherwise from the registration date. A `hekel mizvad` waiver can shift the anchor back for force-majeure delays (hospitalization, miluim, abroad for emergency); request in writing from the local BL branch with documentation
- **Counting rule:** any month with even one day of salaried work counts as a full qualifying month
- **Freelance months do NOT count:** only salaried (shakhir) employment qualifies. Osek Patur and Osek Murshe months are excluded, even if BL was paid
- **Self-employed to salaried trap:** closing an osek and becoming a shakhir for 7 to 10 months is a common rejection scenario; the akhshara count restarts from the first salaried month
- **Military service:** mandatory IDF service months are credited for the qualifying period
- **Parental leave:** months on paid parental leave (dmei leda) count toward the qualifying period; chofesh lelo tashlum (CHALAT) extending leda up to 12 months also does not break the count
- **Sick leave:** months while on paid sick leave from the employer count
- **Reserve duty (miluim):** days in miluim count as work days; long miluim service extends the registration window via hekel mizvad
- **Work abroad counts in only two narrow situations, and the usual list of treaty countries is WRONG for this purpose.** (a) Work abroad **for an Israeli employer under a contract signed in Israel** counts directly. (b) Periods accrued in a country with which Israel has a treaty **in the unemployment branch** can be aggregated on conditions, and that is only **Austria, the Netherlands and Sweden**. Israel has general social-security treaties with many more countries (the US, the UK, Germany, France, Canada, Switzerland and others), but those treaties do NOT cover the unemployment branch. Telling a returning Israeli from the US or the UK to file for aggregation sends them to a refusal. Source: btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/DaysHaxhsharaAvt.aspx (`מדינה שעימה יש לישראל אמנה בענף אבטלה (אוסטריה, הולנד ושבדיה)`)

## Months that are not work months but still count toward the 12

If the claimant is short of 12 salaried months, these count as well. Source: btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/DaysHaxhsharaAvt.aspx.

- **Regular IDF service**: up to 6 months.
- **National or national-civil service**: up to 6 months, but ONLY for someone who completed 24 months of volunteering, or a woman who volunteered at least 6 months and married within 30 days of stopping the service.
- **Miluim** (reserve service).
- **Unpaid leave**: the **first 2 full months** of chal"t for which the employer paid insurance contributions.
- **Paid vacation and holiday days.** But **pidyon chufsha paid on severance of the employment relationship does NOT count**, which is the trap here: the same money counts or does not count depending on why it was paid.
- **Mourning days** that fell inside a period of employment, **even if the employer paid nothing for them**.
- **Sick-leave periods** for which the claimant was entitled to dmei machala as an employee.
- **Periods paid as** dmei pgia be'avoda (work injury), dmei leda, or kitzva le'shmirat herayon.
- **Periods paid as compensation for failure to give prior notice.** Note the asymmetry: that payment BLOCKS benefit for the period it covers, and at the same time COUNTS toward the qualifying period. Agents routinely record only the first half and lose a borderline claimant a month.
- **Work abroad**, in the two narrow situations set out above.

## Extending the 18-month look-back window

The window itself moves back if the claimant did not work during it for any of these reasons. Same source.

| Reason | Extension |
|---|---|
| Vocational training on referral or approval of Sherut HaTaasuka | up to **12 months** |
| Illness or accident for which no dmei machala were paid | up to **6 months** |
| Maternity leave under s. 6 of the Women's Employment Law, where no dmei leda were paid | for that period |
| Shmirat herayon for which no benefit was paid | for that period |
| Unpaid leave after birth under s. 7 of the Women's Employment Law | up to about **a quarter of the months worked**, and never more than **12 months from the birth** |
| Absence from work for reasons outside the employee's control | up to **1 month** |

A claimant who spent a year in an approved course and is told they failed akhshara has almost certainly not had the window extended. Check this before accepting a refusal.

## Age-Specific Rules

| Age at unemployment | Eligibility | Notes |
|---------------------|-------------|-------|
| Under 20 | Only via the closed exception list below | Do NOT tell an under-20 claimant they are simply ineligible |
| 20-67 | Full eligibility | Standard rules apply |
| 67+ | Not eligible for avtala | Claim kitzvat zikna (old age pension) instead |

**Qualifying months only count between age 18 and 67** (btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/zakaut.aspx).

**Under-20 exception list (closed list).** A claimant under 20 may be entitled if any one of these applies:
- discharged from regular IDF service, other than a discharge for "אי התאמה" on grounds of serious misconduct
- completed 24 months of national or national-civil service, or is a bat sherut who stopped after at least 6 months of service and married within 30 days of stopping
- exempted or deferred by the IDF on grounds of health, family, education, hityashvut bithonit, or national-economy needs
- is the sole breadwinner of the family (parents, siblings, spouse), or has a child whose maintenance falls mainly on them

A **na'ar from age 15** may also be entitled in certain cases through the separate "הענקת אבטלה לנער" track. Route the user there rather than closing the question.
Source: btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/under20.aspx

## Reason for Termination Matrix

The 90-day waiting period for resignation runs from the **day work ceased** (`מיום הפסקת העבודה`), not from the registration date, and is calendar days (not work days). The wait delays the start; it does NOT reduce total max days.

| Reason | Wait period | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Fired without cause (pituin) | 0 days | Full benefits from day 1 |
| Made redundant (pituin kalkali) | 0 days | Same as firing |
| End of fixed-term contract | 0 days | Full benefits |
| Employer bankruptcy / dissolution | 0 days | Additionally can claim unpaid wages separately |
| Unpaid leave (chalat) 30+ days, standard | 0 days | Under standard chalat rules |
| Chal"t inside the Shaagat HaAri window **[CLOSED 14.5.2026]** | 0 days | Emergency track; no 5-day deduction; no need to exhaust accrued vacation. Minimum chal"t length: **5 consecutive days ONLY if the chal"t started 28.2.2026 or 1.3.2026** (first two war days); **10 consecutive days** for any chal"t starting later |
| Unpaid leave under 30 days (standard) | Not eligible | Too short to qualify outside the emergency track |
| Resignation (hitpatrut) without justified cause | 90 days | Calendar days, counted from the day work ceased |
| Resignation with justified cause (hitpatrut b'din mefuteret) | 0 days | See justified causes below |
| Refused a suitable job offer from Sherut HaTaasuka | 90 days waiting + 30 days deducted from max-day total | Two separate penalties: 90-day delay AND 30-day reduction in max days |
| Dismissed without lawful prior notice | See notes | Entitlement is examined from the last payment day, or from the end of 30 days from the start of the notice, whichever is EARLIER. That paid period counts as qualifying period and carries no benefit. Source: btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/nesibothafsakatavoda.aspx. **Note there is no "fired for cause" waiting period.** An earlier version of this file stated a 30-day wait for dismissal for criminal conduct or gross negligence; BL's page draws no such distinction and pays a dismissed claimant from the first day of attendance. Do not reintroduce it |
| Retirement before pension age, employer-initiated or under an early-retirement scheme | 0 days | Entitled from the first day of attendance |
| Voluntary retirement before pension age | 90 days | Treated as resignation. A woman who retires voluntarily **after age 62** is treated the same way: no benefit for the first 90 days from the retirement date, unless the retirement had justified cause, in which case she is entitled from the first day of attendance |
| Employee-initiated chal"t | **Not eligible at all** | "מי שיצא לחל\"ת מיוזמתו (גם אם החל\"ת מוצדק) - לא יהיה זכאי לדמי אבטלה." This is a disqualification, not a delay |
| Teacher on a shnat shabbaton | Not eligible for that period | They may not work more than a third of a post, so they are not available for offered work |

**Employer-initiated chal"t: the vacation-day offset.** Remaining vacation days at the employer are set off first. BL's own example: a 50-day chal"t with 20 vacation days left pays benefit from **day 21**. The vacation balance does not shorten the chal"t itself, and the employee does not have to actually consume the vacation days. During the chal"t the employer may not call the employee in even for single days; if the employee returns to work, benefit stops from the day of return.
Source: btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/nesibothafsakatavoda.aspx

**Severance pay (pitzuei piturin) interaction.** Legal pitzuim do NOT delay or reduce avtala. What Bituach Leumi does postpone the benefit for is vacation pay paid under the Annual Leave Law, a collective agreement or an employment contract, and payment in lieu of prior notice (up to one month). Note: National Insurance Law s. 174 is NOT this rule, it is the preferred-employment grant.

## Justified Cause for Resignation (hitpatrut b'din mefuteret)

These are the causes Bituach Leumi itself publishes. Each one, if proved, restores entitlement from the first day of registration instead of after 90 days. Supporting documents must be attached to the claim.

1. **Har'a muchashit** in the conditions of employment, or circumstances arising at the workplace that made it impossible to carry on working
2. **Health**, either the claimant's own or that of a family member. The family members BL names are spouse, parent, child, grandchild, brother or sister
3. **Distance.** A change of residence or of workplace where the distance between the new home and the workplace exceeds **60 km**, or **40 km** for a mother of a child under 7
4. **Sexual harassment** at the workplace
5. **Working outside one's trade.** A holder of a te'udat miktzoa who was working in a job that does not match the trade and resigns in order to work in it
6. **Staying in a shelter** for victims of domestic violence or for battered women
7. **Resignation from a NEW job** held for up to 6 months, where the claimant had not yet realised an earlier entitlement
8. **Resignation from a SECOND job**, where the claimant held two jobs of similar scope, resigned from one of them with justification, and stayed in the second for up to 3 months

Source: btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/nesibothafsakatavoda.aspx

**What is NOT on BL's published list.** Earlier versions of this skill stated a "25% or more pay cut over a 6-month window" test and a requirement that a medical letter come from an occupational physician rather than a GP. Neither appears on any reachable BL page. They may reflect branch practice, but do not present them to a user as rules. Frame a pay cut as a har'a muchashit argument under limb 1, evidenced by payslips, and tell the user BL decides.

**Related labour-law point, not a BL rule.** Dismissal in the 60-day period after returning from maternity leave, without a Labour Ministry permit, is unlawful under s.9 of the Women's Employment Law. That claimant registers as **fired**, and may have a separate labour-court claim.

## Not insured for unemployment at all (statutory exclusion list)

These people are outside the unemployment branch entirely. No amount of salaried months helps them, so run this check BEFORE counting the qualifying period.

- **Osek (self-employed).**
- **A controlling shareholder in a closely-held company (בעל שליטה בחברת מעטים)** is excluded **even when he is employed there as a salaried employee and contributions were paid.** This is the single most-missed exclusion: the claimant has 12 clean salaried months and is still refused.
- **A kibbutz member or a member of a moshav shitufi**, unless they work as an employee outside the collective.
- **A soldier in regular service, and anyone in national or civil service**, for the duration of that service.
- **Students and yeshiva students** who are not working as salaried employees.

Source: btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/zakaut.aspx ("מי לא זכאי לדמי אבטלה?")

**Insured despite an employer that owes no Israeli contributions.** An employee whose employer is not an Israeli resident, and an employee of a foreign diplomatic or consular service, CAN be entitled although the employer pays nothing; they pay their own contributions. An employee working abroad for an Israeli employer under a contract signed in Israel may also be entitled. Do not turn these claimants away.

## Disqualifications

| Reason | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Working for spouse / parent / child / sibling without arms-length employment | Not eligible unless the employment relationship is proved genuine. In practice BL looks for a dated employment contract, payslips predating any dispute, a bank-transfer wage trail rather than cash, a third-party witness to actual work, and proof that tax, BL and pension were paid on time. **The circular number sometimes quoted for this rule could not be verified against a reachable BTL page, so do not cite one to the user.** |
| Self-employed (osek) for most of the qualifying window | Fails standard tkufat akhshara, and an osek is not insured for unemployment at all |
| Already receiving a competing BL benefit | Cannot stack; BL evaluates per case. The specific 75% disability threshold is unverified, check with BL |
| Already receiving dmei leda | Avtala blocked for the leda period; switch carefully when leda ends |
| Not an Israeli resident | Residency is decided by CENTRE OF LIFE (`מרכז חייו בישראל`: permanent home, where the family is, where the children are schooled, main workplace, place of study), not by a day count. An earlier version of this file stated a bright-line "more than 6 months abroad" test; no source supports it. A temporary resident holding a permanent or temporary residence permit also qualifies |
| Foreign worker on permit (most cases) | Generally not entitled, with narrow exceptions for some Palestinian workers and caregivers |
| Under investigation for benefit fraud | Suspended until resolved |

## Reporting Obligations

Once approved, the claimant must:
- Attend every scheduled reporting meeting at Sherut HaTaasuka
- Apply to the minimum number of jobs the lishka sets for each reporting period. **UNSOURCED FIGURE:** the "2 to 3 per period" often quoted is not published; read the quota off the assignment rather than quoting a number
- Accept any "suitable offer" under National Insurance Law s. 165 (**but see "The refusal penalty is not universal" at the end of this file before quoting the penalty to a woman on the 300-day track**): job type or training/education match, wage at least the dmei avtala otherwise due, no change of home (60 km rule). The first two tests relax by age: under 25 after 14 days, 25-28 after 30 days, 28-35 after 60 days
- Refusing an assigned vocational-training course (kursim mukarim, maslulei mahalehet) is normally treated like refusing a job offer: 90-day waiting penalty + 30-day deduction. **Two published exceptions on the women's 300-day track, see the end of this file**
- Report any side income immediately. Income from work, self-employment or a pension is deducted from the benefit; s. 176 deducts the average daily income from the daily benefit
- Failure to report side income = fraud = retroactive clawback + possible criminal liability


## Shaagat HaAri 2026 emergency chal"t regime -- detailed rules [WINDOW CLOSED 14.5.2026]

**Status: closed.** The defining period ran 28 Feb to 14 Apr 2026, extendable only to 14 May 2026. Nothing in this section applies to leave or unemployment beginning after that date; those claimants fall under the standard 12-of-18 rules. Kol-Zchut notes the framework can be re-activated by order for a future emergency through the end of 2027, so treat it as a dormant standby mechanism.

**Employer-initiated vs employee-initiated.** Most halat under this regime is employer-initiated and routine. Employee-initiated halat IS allowed but raises a justification question at BL: a claimant who pushed for the leave themselves without documented cause (medical, family, dependent-care) may be treated as voluntary cessation and pulled into the 90-day waiting period despite the emergency framework. Walk the user through their circumstances carefully.

**Working during the halat even partially disqualifies.** Even one day of paid work for the halat employer (or any other employer) inside the halat window may void the claim for the entire halat period, not just the day worked. Vacation pay (חופשה בתשלום) and sick pay (דמי מחלה) received during the halat have the same effect: they signal the employment relationship was not actually suspended.

**Additional income offsets (not full disqualifiers).** Self-employed (עוסק) income or pension payments received during the halat trigger a קיזוז (offset) against the daily benefit, not a hard disqualification. Bituach Leumi deducts the income from the benefit for that period.

**Self-employed who is ALSO salaried (חצי-חצי).** A claimant who runs an עוסק *and* draws a regular salary can claim dmei avtala on the **salary component only**, with a רואה חשבון אישור attesting to income from both sources for the relevant 6-month base period. The osek months still don't count toward akhshara (Gotcha #6 applies), but the salaried portion is paid normally.

**Recurring claimants (מובטלים חוזרים).** The Shaagat HaArie act extends and eases entitlement for claimants who previously exhausted avtala benefits, including a shortened re-qualifying window and an extension to the 12-month consumption clock for those affected by the war period. Specific extension parameters are set by minister order; check btl.gov.il/StateOfEmergency/ShaagatHari for the current calibration before promising days.

**Workers aged 67+ get a special grant, not avtala.** Claimants above the avtala age ceiling receive a dedicated emergency grant under the same act in lieu of regular dmei avtala. The grant is one-time, age-conditioned, and filed through the Bituach Leumi Shaagat HaArie portal (not the standard avtala flow). Verify the current amount on the BL emergency page before quoting.

Verify the final extension date and any further parameter changes at btl.gov.il/StateOfEmergency/ShaagatHari/Pages/halat-shaagatHari1.aspx before quoting numbers; the framework was amended multiple times between March and May 2026.

### Vocational Training (hachshara miktzoit) During Unemployment

Claimants placed in an approved Sherut HaTaasuka training program (kursim mukarim, maslulei mahalehet) keep receiving avtala throughout the program, and may receive an enhanced stipend (dmei kiyum) in addition. Days spent in training do NOT consume max-days the same way active job-seeking days do, verify with the case worker before assuming.

Refusing an assigned training course is normally treated like refusing a job offer: 90-day waiting penalty + 30-day deduction, **subject to the two women's-track exceptions at the end of this file**. A participant with fewer than 12 years of schooling whose own maximum is under 138 days is paid for up to **138 days**. Encourage the user to attend.

### Stacking with Hashlamat Hachnasa (Income Supplementation)

Avtala benefits below the household subsistence threshold can be topped up with hashlamat hachnasa for low earners with dependents. File via Bituach Leumi form 5320. The amount follows the hashlamat hachnasa rules and BL publishes no typical supplement figure, so do not quote one. This is not automatic; the claimant has to apply separately.

### Reservists, Returning Israelis, Foreign Treaty Workers

- **Laid off during or shortly after long miluim service.** Hok Khayalim Meshukhrarim s. 41a forbids dismissal during miluim and 30 days after. The avtala registration window is extended for the miluim period; a hekel mizvad waiver is granted on request.
- **Worked abroad recently.** Work abroad counts only (a) for an Israeli employer under a contract signed in Israel, or (b) by aggregating periods from a country with which Israel has a treaty **in the unemployment branch**, which is only **Austria, the Netherlands and Sweden**. Israel's general social-security treaties (US, UK, Germany, France, Canada, Switzerland and others) do NOT cover unemployment. Source: btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/DaysHaxhsharaAvt.aspx Bring the foreign country's social-insurance statement. Do not quote a form number, see `application-forms.md`.
- **Foreign workers on permit.** Generally not entitled, with narrow exceptions for some Palestinian workers and caregivers.

### Maternity Leave Edge Cases

- Months on dmei leda count toward akhshara.
- The 60-day post-leda period is a protected period under Hok Avoda Nashim s. 9. Firing during this window without a Labor Ministry permit is illegal, register as fired AND consider a separate labor-court complaint.
- Extending leda with chofesh lelo tashlum (CHALAT) up to 12 months does not break tkufat akhshara.
- Drawing dmei leda blocks avtala for the leda period; switch carefully when leda ends.

### Sickness or Disability During Avtala

- Cannot draw both avtala and dmei machala simultaneously. Report sickness to BL within the reporting cadence; switch to sick-pay status. Some sick days still consume avtala max-days.
- A competing BL benefit can block avtala, case by case. The specific 75% disability threshold is unverified.


## The refusal penalty is not universal: the two women's sub-bands differ

The standard penalty for refusing suitable work, training, hishtalmut or hasava miktzoit is **90 days with no benefit from the day of refusal, every time, plus 30 days off the quota** (btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/pay.aspx). Two published exceptions change it:

- **A woman aged 57 to 60 on the 300-day track** who refuses vocational training **in the first 175 days** gets the standard 90 + 30 (leaving 270 days). If she refuses **from day 176 onward**, she forfeits entitlement for the **entire remaining balance**, which is far harsher than the standard penalty.
- **A woman aged 60 to 67 on the 300-day track** who refuses work or vocational training **continues to receive benefit as usual.** The 90 + 30 penalty does not apply to her at all. Telling her otherwise is the most common way this track is got wrong.

Source: btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/zecoyot-nasim.aspx

## Sick days, miluim and mourning days during unemployment

- **Sick days:** up to **30** sick days are payable inside the unemployment period and **are deducted from the quota**. No payment for the first 2 sick days unless the illness lasted at least 12 consecutive days. The doctor's certificate must cover Shabbatot and holidays; sick days recorded on a Shabbat are unpaid; a child's illness does not qualify.
- **Miluim:** attendance days pay unemployment benefit, reserve days pay tagmul miluim, and the tagmul will be at least the unemployment benefit that would otherwise have been due.
- **Mourning days:** an exemption from attendance is possible with benefit still paid; the length depends on religion and degree of kinship.
- **Abroad:** days abroad with no attendance are unpaid. On return, benefit resumes per attendance until the remaining days in the period are used.

Source: btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/Avtala_att.aspx and .../pay.aspx
