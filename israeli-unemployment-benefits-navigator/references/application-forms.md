# Dmei Avtala Application Forms and Portals

All official forms, portals, and contact points for Israeli unemployment claims. Procedural windows are 2026 values; verify the current set on btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment before relying on them.

## Primary Portals

| Portal | URL | Purpose |
|--------|-----|---------|
| Sherut HaTaasuka (Employment Service) | https://www.taasuka.gov.il | Register as job seeker, attend reporting, job search |
| Bituach Leumi (National Insurance) | https://www.btl.gov.il/Pages/default.aspx | File unemployment claim, check status, appeal decisions |
| Government Login (zehut digitalit) | https://www.gov.il | Unified digital ID for all online services |

## Required Documents

Gather these before starting the online application:

1. **Teudat Zehut** (ID card) + sefach (appendix)
2. **Termination letter** (mikhtav piturin) signed by employer, stating the termination date
3. **Form 100** is the wage data the employer transmits to Bituach Leumi. Check it was transmitted; if it was not, attach an employer confirmation of the employment period and wage, or 12 payslips out of the last 18. Form 126 is the employer's annual report to the assessing officer and is not on the BTL document list
4. **Last 6 payslips** (tlushei maskoret)
5. **Bank account details** (snif, cheshbon) for benefit deposits
6. **Marriage certificate** if claiming as married
7. **Birth certificates** of dependent children
8. **For resigners claiming justified cause**: supporting documentation matching whichever of the eight published grounds applies (medical evidence, a harassment complaint, proof of the new home's distance, a professional certificate, a shelter confirmation, etc.). **Retracted:** an earlier version of this file said a regular GP letter is not accepted and that the letter must come from a `rofeh tasukati`. That requirement appears on no reachable BL page. Attach the best medical evidence available and let BL decide; do not tell a claimant holding a GP letter that their claim will fail
9. **For returning Israelis**: the foreign social-insurance statement, plus whatever aggregation confirmation the branch asks for. **Do not quote a form number**, see the forms table above, and note that unemployment-branch aggregation covers only Austria, the Netherlands and Sweden

## Bituach Leumi Forms

The online unified form replaces most paper forms, but here are the key forms that may still be requested:

| Form | Hebrew name | When |
|------|-------------|------|
| **Form T1500** (main claim, online) | תביעה לדמי אבטלה | The unemployment claim itself. Two routes: already registered at Sherut HaTaasuka, file the online claim with documents attached; not yet registered, file the single joint online form through מערכת הזדהות לאומית, which doubles as the initial Employment Service registration. **File within 12 months of first attendance**; filing later pays only for the 12 months preceding the filing date. File even with documents missing. Source: btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/submit.aspx |
| Employer confirmation | אישור המעסיק | A signed and stamped employer confirmation of the reason and date employment ended. **UNVERIFIED FORM NUMBER:** this is often called "Form 1514", but no reachable BTL page names a form number for it, and the substitute route sometimes described for an uncooperative employer (a tatzhir plus payslips plus bank statements) is not published anywhere either, nor is the circular number quoted for it. Do not give the user a form number for this. If the employer will not sign, file within the 12-month window with what you have and call *6050 |
| Bank details | פרטי חשבון בנק | **UNVERIFIED FORM NUMBER:** often called "Form 5040", but BTL asks for bank details inside the online claim with no form number on any reachable page. Do not quote a form number |
| Form 5320 (income supplementation) | בקשה להשלמת הכנסה | When low avtala plus dependents qualifies for hashlamat hachnasa stacking |
| Treaty periods confirmation | אישור תקופות ביטוח לפי אמנה | **UNVERIFIED FORM NUMBER:** often called "בל/627"; no reachable BTL page names it. Note also that the unemployment-branch treaty covers only **Austria, the Netherlands and Sweden** (see below), so this route is far narrower than it is usually described |
| Appeal (irur) | ערעור על החלטת ביטוח לאומי | When appealing a denial or underpayment |

**Form 126 is NOT part of an unemployment claim.** It is the employer's annual report to the assessing officer. BTL's published document list asks the claimant to confirm the employer transmitted **Form 100** (the wage data) and, failing that, to attach an employer confirmation of the employment period and wages, or 12 payslips out of the last 18. Do not send the user chasing Form 126.

## Sherut HaTaasuka Process

1. **Online registration** at taasuka.gov.il (preferred)
2. **In-person follow-up at the local branch within 14 days** of online registration. The online step is NOT valid without this. Bring original teudat zehut + sefach + all docs
3. **Initial interview** with a caseworker to set up a job-seeking plan
4. **Scheduled reporting days** (yemei harshama) assigned by the lishka. **UNSOURCED CADENCE:** "every 2 weeks" is commonly repeated but is not published; the lishka sets it per claimant, so read it off the assignment
5. **Job search quota**: a minimum number of suitable job applications per reporting period. **UNSOURCED FIGURE:** the "2 to 3 per period" often quoted is not published; the lishka sets the quota
6. **Acceptance of offers**: refusing a "suitable" offer triggers a penalty. The suitability test is the one in **s. 165 of the National Insurance Law**: the job is of the main type worked in the last 3 years or matches the claimant's training or education, the wage is at least the dmei avtala otherwise due, and it does not require a change of home (a **60 km** test, not a 60-minute commute test). The first two limbs relax by **AGE**, not on day 60 for everyone: under 25 after 14 days, 25-28 after 30 days, 28-35 after 60 days.
   **Retracted:** an earlier version of this file stated a "salary within 25% of prior wage, commute under 60 minutes, profession within one degree, relaxes after day 60" test and attributed it to a "post-2024 BL clarification". No such test and no such clarification exists. Do not reintroduce it.
7. **Vocational training** (kursim mukarim, maslulei mahalehet): when assigned, attendance is mandatory. Refusing normally costs 90 days plus a 30-day quota deduction, **but see the two published women's-track exceptions in `eligibility-rules.md`: a woman aged 60-67 on the 300-day track keeps her benefit in full when she refuses, and a woman aged 57-60 who refuses from day 176 forfeits her entire remaining balance.** While in training, avtala continues at the full rate, and a participant with fewer than 12 years of schooling whose own maximum is under 138 days is paid for up to **138 days**

## Appeal Process (Irur)

If the claim is denied or underpaid, there are three escalation steps.

**UNVERIFIED DEADLINES.** The 6-month, 12-month and 60-day windows below are widely repeated but could not be confirmed against any reachable source: s. 396 of the National Insurance Law leaves them to regulations that were not reachable. Treat them as indicative, tell the user to confirm the exact deadline on their own decision letter (which states it) or by calling *6050, and never let a user rely on this table alone to decide they still have time. A limitation period is the one thing in this skill where being approximately right is the same as being wrong.

| Step | Window | Where | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|-------|
| Informal review (va'adat tvi'ot) | 6 months from the decision | Local BL branch in writing | Attach new evidence; non-binding internal review |
| Formal appeal | 12 months from the original decision | Beit Din Ezorit L'Avoda (Regional Labor Court) | Can self-represent for simple cases; lawyer recommended for complex ones |
| Appeal of a Va'adat Ararim ruling specifically | 60 days from the Va'adat Ararim decision | Beit Din Ezorit L'Avoda | This shorter window applies ONLY when escalating from a formal Va'adat Ararim ruling, not from the original BL decision. Very easy to confuse with the 12-month window |

**Free legal aid** for low-income claimants runs through the **Legal Aid Department (האגף לסיוע משפטי) of the Ministry of Justice**, which handles National Insurance and labour-court matters. It is NOT the Sanegoria HaTziburit (Public Defender), which is a criminal-defence body and does not take these cases. Kolzchut has online self-representation guidance.

## Reporting Income While Receiving Benefits

If the claimant takes on part-time or temporary work while drawing dmei avtala:

- **Must report immediately** via the Bituach Leumi portal or caseworker
- **Benefit reduction**: BL compares per day, not by a flat percentage. Gross monthly wage divided by the days actually worked gives an average daily wage; below the daily benefit you get the difference for those days, at or above it you get nothing for those days. Source: btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/incomes.aspx
- **Avtala days used**: partial workdays still count against the max days. BTL's own tip: if you worked only a few days at a wage below the daily benefit, ask Sherut HaTaasuka **not to record unemployment days** for those days, so no offset is applied at all
- **Failure to report** = fraud = retroactive clawback + possible criminal liability

## Stacking Other BL Benefits

| Benefit | Stack with avtala? |
|---------|-------------------|
| Hashlamat hachnasa (form 5320) | Yes, for low earners with dependents. The amount is set by the hashlamat hachnasa rules, not by a fixed supplement; BL publishes no typical figure, so do not quote one |
| Dmei lida | No, blocks avtala for the leda period |
| Kitzvat nechut klalit | **UNVERIFIED THRESHOLD.** A 75% general-disability threshold is widely repeated as blocking avtala, with partial disability below it sometimes allowed. Neither could be confirmed on any reachable BTL page. Tell the user to check with BL rather than treating either as a rule. What IS published: a loss-of-earning-capacity benefit that does NOT require retirement from work is not treated as a retirement pension and is not deducted (btl.gov.il/benefits/Unemployment/Pages/incomes.aspx) |
| Kiztavat zikna | No, applies after age 67 instead |
| Tagmulei miluim | Yes, miluim service days do not consume avtala max-days |
