# Domain checklist: municipal internal audit report

Anchor for the Expert Review. Built 2026-10-01 from the Municipalities Ordinance (ss. 149C,
166(aa), 167, 170 to 170C1A, 171, 334A), the Local Councils Ordinance (ss. 13B, 13D, 13E), the
State Comptroller Law (ss. 20, 21A to 21C, 28), the Local Authorities (Public Complaints
Commissioner) Law 2008, the Water and Sewage Corporations Law (ss. 70, 71, 138), the 2018
auditor-office regulations, and HCJ 7805/00 (Aloni).

## Must cover (core)

- Authority type and what is imported for local and regional councils (13B, 13D, 13E).
- Audit scope: 170A(a) duties; audited municipal bodies under 170A(b) (religious council,
  one-tenth budget test per budget year, management-appointment limb); the water and sewage
  corporation carve-out (Water Corporations Law 138).
- Work plan: three sources, two-topic cap on committee demands, auditor sets methods (170A(c)-(d)).
- Access powers (170B): documents and explanations within the set period, database access,
  invitation to council and committee meetings; refusal recorded as a scope limitation.
- Report contents (170C(a)): summary, deficiencies, recommendations.
- Due process before finalising: hearing and inspection rights of the audited party (Aloni).
- Timeline (170C(a)-(e)): 1 April, copy to committee, 3/2/2 months each from the actual prior
  step, fallback A (5 months from delivery to committee), fallback B (7 months from submission
  to the mayor, auditor delivers copies).
- Publication ban (170C(f)), permission route, 334A penalty, Aloni limit on early publication,
  undefined "date set for submission to the council".
- Evidentiary status (170C1).
- Direct referral to the State Comptroller of a suspected offence under Penal Law Ch.E Art.B by
  the mayor or the legal advisor (170C(g)).
- Deficiency-correction team: 170C1A(b)-(d) clocks; regional council excluded (13D).
- State Comptroller report track (SC Law 20(d), 21A(c), 21B(a), 21C) and the audit
  committee's duty to discuss those reports (149C(a)).
- Public-complaints commissioner: default appointment of the auditor, separate 1 May report,
  different evidentiary rule (Complaints Law ss. 2, 15, 16).

## Should cover (advanced)

- Audit committee composition and chair rules (149C(b)-(c)); quarterly meeting duty (166(aa)).
- Office staffing and budget minimums for a municipality (2018 regulations, full tables in
  `auditor-office-standards.md`); 170A(f) council discusses the proposal as submitted.
- Internal Audit Law 1992 status: not binding on a local authority by statute; religious
  council is a public body under it.
- Auditor independence: dismissal by three-quarters of council members after a hearing
  (171(b)(2), (c)); staff take professional instructions only from the auditor (170(f)).
- Auditing elected officials insofar as their acts relate to the municipality (Aloni).
- Part-time local-council auditor and completing the post (13E(a)-(b)).

## Out of scope (explicit)

- Appointment procedure, tender and eligibility of a new auditor (167(c) to (c2)): the skill
  serves a sitting auditor writing a report. Reviewed 2026-10-01: a user asking "am I eligible"
  is not this skill's user; kept out, but the dismissal protection is covered because it
  bears on writing an unwelcome report.
- The founding-order (צו הכינון) provisions for local-council auditors: they vary by council, so the skill routes the user to their order rather than stating
  rules it cannot verify per council. Reviewed 2026-10-01.
- Professional audit standards (IIA, the Association of Local Authority Auditors guidelines):
  not statutory for a local authority; methodology is the auditor's own discretion under
  170A(d). Reviewed 2026-10-01.
- Ministry of the Interior administrative practice on sending copies of reports: no statutory
  deadline was found in the Ordinance, so the skill does not state one. Reviewed 2026-10-01.
- State Comptroller audits themselves, private-sector internal audit, external
  financial-statement audits (see the description's Do NOT clause).
