# Minimum staff and budget of a municipal auditor's office

Source: Municipalities Regulations (Standards and Annual Budget for the Office of a Municipal
Auditor), 2018 (תקנות העיריות (תקנים ותקציב שנתי ללשכת מבקר עירייה), התשע"ח-2018), made
under sections 170(e), 170A(e) and 347 of the Municipalities Ordinance. In force from
1 June 2019.

**Municipalities only.** These regulations are not imported into the Local Councils
Ordinance; for a local or regional council the founding order governs the office budget.

Definitions (regulation 1):
- "Number of residents" has its meaning under section 19 of the Ordinance.
- The office's annual budget **includes** the salaries of the auditor and the office staff.
- The municipality's annual budget is its regular annual budget **excluding** income to cover
  the accumulated deficit and income recorded for arnona discounts.

## How the budget is handled

Under section 170A(f) the finance committee and the council discuss the office's budget and
staffing proposals **as the auditor submitted them**, as part of the annual budget debate.
Under section 170(e) the mayor appoints office staff with the auditor's consent, and under
170(f) they take professional instructions from the auditor only.
