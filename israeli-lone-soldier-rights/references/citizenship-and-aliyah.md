# Aliyah Intersections for Oleh Lone Soldiers

Companion to SKILL.md Step 9. Citizenship, the absorption basket and general aliyah benefits are outside this skill; route them to the aliyah skills. Kol Zchut defines an oleh under Chok HaShvut ("עולה מוגדר לפי חוק השבות").

| Intersection | What the sources say |
|---|---|
| Aliyah-Ministry lone-soldier grant | 100% of a private's base salary (150% for olim from Ethiopia), on top of the IDF grant; within 5 years of aliyah on enlistment day (10 for Ethiopia); renewed yearly; exceptions committee for later cases (Kol Zchut). SKILL.md Step 3. |
| Olim rent assistance | A lone soldier also entitled to rent assistance as a new immigrant gets an addition to the IDF rent participation (Kol Zchut). SKILL.md Step 4. |
| Pikadon from abroad | Pikadon money can be used only in Israel (hachvana DepositUpTo5). A discharged soldier who returns abroad should not expect to spend it there. |
| Returning minors, ezrach oleh, toshav chozer | Eligible for the Aliyah-Ministry grant if recognized by the IDF as a lone soldier, even without an independent Teudat Oleh (Kol Zchut). |
