# Operational Items That Decide Whether Benefits Arrive

Companion to SKILL.md Step 8.5. Where no source publishes a rule, the row says so and the agent must not invent one.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Bank details | hachvana pays the rent refund, and the Pikadon balance left after 5 years, to the bank account in the personal area; an update is confirmed by SMS within 48 hours. The Aliyah-Ministry grant asks for a bank confirmation. |
| Arnona | Kol Zchut lists an exemption among the ACTIVE-DUTY housing benefits. No source says it continues after discharge. hachvana's post-discharge rent benefit expressly excludes arnona. |
| Electricity | 50% off the first 400 kWh a month at the domestic rate, only for a lone soldier receiving the IDF rent participation; about 129 NIS a month at most (Kol Zchut, January 2026). Automatic via an IDF file to the electricity company, matched on the account holder's name and ID number, so the contract must be in the soldier's name. Missing discount: call 103. A soldier who switched to a private supplier keeps the discount through that supplier. No source says it continues after discharge. |
| Reserve duty | Per hachvana's page for lone soldiers in reserves: a soldier recognized as a lone soldier (muvhak, chaser oref or yatom) through discharge is treated as a lone soldier in reserves for 10 years from discharge; the Iron Swords definition also requires more than 60 cumulative reserve days during the war. Route the details to `israeli-miluim-manager`. |
| Marriage during service | Per the IDF benefits page, about 1,080 NIS special grant plus three months of continued boded benefits. Discharge after those three months ends eligibility for discharged-lone-soldier benefits (SKILL.md Step 5). |

Sources: kolzchut.org.il (סיוע בהוצאות דיור לחיילים בודדים, הנחה בחשבון חשמל לחיילים בודדים), hachvana.mod.gov.il (SingleSolders/Rent.aspx, SingleSolders/Lone-Soldiers-in-Reserve-Duty.aspx, DepositUpTo5), the IDF lone-soldier economic-benefits page.
