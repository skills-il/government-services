# Diaspora and Israeli NGO Directory for Lone Soldiers

Companion to SKILL.md Step 8. These organizations work alongside the IDF and the MoD. Route lone soldiers to them IN ADDITION to the official applications. Programs change; each row states only what the organization's own page or Kol Zchut says, and the agent should send the user to the site for anything current.

## Nefesh B'Nefesh + FIDF Lone Soldiers Program

- URL: https://www.nbn.org.il/nbnlsp/
- Its benefits page (https://www.nbn.org.il/nbnlsp/lone-soldier-benefits/) keeps a lone-soldier benefits list and a financial table dated February 2024, and lists the IDF lone-soldier department (Merkaz Bodedim) lines: 1111 from Israel, +972-3-5691000 from abroad.
- Pre-aliyah and pre-enlistment guidance for olim; resources for parents.

## Friends of the IDF (FIDF)

- URL: https://www.fidf.org/lone-soldier-support/
- Its page lists, with NBN: a 24-hour call center, advocacy with government agencies, holiday meals and Errands Day, support for parents, a small financial safety net at the start of service, holiday gift vouchers, flights home, an annual Fun Day, and housing ("Warm Homes").
- Kol Zchut: FIDF is one of the funders of the "מתגעגעים הביתה" flights project.

## Aguda LeMaan HaChayal (Association for the Wellbeing of Israel's Soldiers)

- Runs the Beit HaChayal network (Kol Zchut): permanent residence, free lodging on leave and holidays, up to 3 months after discharge.
- Organizes the "מתגעגעים הביתה" flights, funded by FIDF, ASI Canada, the International Fellowship (הקרן לידידות) and El Al's frequent-flyer club (Kol Zchut). In special cases, at the IDF's request, it flies family members to Israel.
- Co-runs the Alach / "יחד למען החייל" apartments with the IDF.

## Garin Tzabar

- URL: https://garintzabar.org/
- Aliyah-and-enlistment preparation program by the Israeli Scouts (Tzofim). Kol Zchut links its English lone-soldier benefits page. Intake terms change between cohorts; check the site.

## Lone Soldier Center in Memory of Michael Levin

- URL: https://www.lonesoldiercenter.com/
- Independent Israel-side organization for lone soldiers during and after service. Check the site for current programs.
- (tzevetmikey.com is a SEPARATE organization, Tzevet Mikey, a pre-army training program; do not confuse the two.)

## Mahal volunteers

- http://www.mahal-idf-volunteers.org/ is a voluntary guide site, by its own statement not an official State of Israel site.
- No source we could verify states Mahal volunteers' lone-soldier recognition or post-discharge rights. Have the volunteer confirm recognition with the Rakezet Tash and post-discharge rent eligibility with hachvana (`*5266`).

## When to route to which organization

| Situation | Start with |
|---|---|
| Oleh from North America, before aliyah | NBN + FIDF |
| Pre-army structured cohort | Garin Tzabar |
| Lodging on leave, after discharge, flights | Aguda LeMaan HaChayal via the Rakezet Tash |
| Community during and after service | Lone Soldier Center |
| Mahal volunteer | Rakezet Tash + `*5266`, then the guide site |
| Mental health during or after service | `israeli-mental-health-navigator` |
