# Additional Lone-Soldier Benefits That Are Easy to Miss

Companion to SKILL.md Step 5.5. Sources: the Kol Zchut "חיילים בודדים" portal and its linked pages, the IDF lone-soldier economic-benefits page, and hachvana. Where a row says "listed on the hub", Kol Zchut gives the title and its own page holds the conditions; route the user there and to the Rakezet Tash rather than inventing details.

## During service

| Benefit | Conditions as published |
|---|---|
| Early release before Shabbat or a holiday (יציאה מוקדמת) | Order §11ה: every lone soldier leaving for a weekend or holiday may leave in time to reach home by 12:00. Kol Zchut narrows it to a lone soldier in a closed unit, or serving more than 50 km from home (Kol Zchut) |
| Subsistence pay (דמי כלכלה) | From the first day of a sick leave at home of 3 days or more; increased by 50% for a soldier who receives it regularly (no army kitchen on base, or medical reasons); also paid during a special leave taken for financial reasons (Kol Zchut) |
| Calling card abroad | For a lone soldier who made aliyah after age 16 and whose parents are abroad: 60 minutes a month to all destinations. Minutes do not roll over and unused months are not refunded retroactively (Kol Zchut) |
| "כוכבים" equipment credit | Credit at listed retailers, sent by message about two weeks before enlistment. IDF page: 215 before enlistment; a month after enlistment 845 (combat) or 335 (combat support); after a year 205 or 145; in total 1,265 or 695 |
| Alach / "יחד למען החייל" apartments | Furnished, usually 4 rooms for 6 soldiers, no rent or taxes, vacate on discharge; residents get 400 NIS a month in vouchers instead of the 150 NIS (Kol Zchut + IDF page) |
| Psychometric-course funding, private-work permit | Listed on the hub; conditions on each page |
| Grant for an oleh lone soldier in crisis | Listed on the hub; conditions on its page |
| Glasses and hearing aids | The hub lists subsidized hearing aids and symbolic-price glasses programs; conditions on each page |
| Aliyah-Ministry monthly grant | See SKILL.md Step 3 |

## After discharge

| Benefit | Conditions as published |
|---|---|
| Beit HaChayal | Up to 3 months free after discharge (hachvana), deducted from the rent-assistance period (Kol Zchut). See SKILL.md Step 6 |
| Mechina | Full tuition plus a monthly living stipend within 10 years of discharge (hachvana) |
| Psychometric | Refund of a psychometric course and test for discharged lone soldiers (hachvana) |
| Scholarships | קרן הישג and קרן גרוס, listed on the hub |
| Programs | The hub lists הבית של בנג'י (guidance before discharge), מנטור לחיים, למרחב (young adults without family backing), גג לחייל המשוחרר (housing for students in the Haifa area), and the כנפיים program of the Jewish Agency and Keren Miraj |
| hachvana services | Personal counselling and a Telegram channel for discharged lone soldiers |
