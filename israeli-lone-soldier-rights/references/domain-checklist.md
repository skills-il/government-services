# Domain Coverage Checklist, israeli-lone-soldier-rights

Generated: 2026-05-18; refreshed 2026-10-07 via research on hachvana.mod.gov.il, kolzchut.org.il, momentum4u.org, mitgaisim.idf.il, nbn.org.il, garintzabar.org.

## Must cover (core)

- [x] **Three eligibility categories (muvhak / chaser oref mishpachti / yatom)** as Kol Zchut defines them, including the ex-Haredi and foster-care sub-cases of chaser oref, and the rule that a keva soldier (or mandatory service under keva terms) is NOT a lone soldier
- [x] **Aka recognition forms**, ONE FORM PER PHASE: a pre-enlistment candidate fills **Form 7304** at the Rakezet Tash's initiative after raising the status at the Lishkat HaGiyus interview; a soldier already serving fills **Form 62** (שאלון הכרה) in two copies in the unit. Form 7304 is NOT internal-only. (Corrected 2026-08-26 against kolzchut הגשת בקשה להכרה כחייל בודד; an earlier cycle had these reversed.)
- [x] **Hotlines**, IDF service line 1111, Soldiers' Ombudsman 03-6977374, 24-hour lone-soldier line 03-7375200 (all Kol Zchut), NBN+FIDF 24-hour call center
- [x] **Active-duty monthly grant**, 100% of the base salary of a turai (private) in mandatory service, paid with the regular salary, automatic upon recognition. Kol Zchut publishes the mechanic with no NIS figure; the 601.2 NIS/month figure comes from the IDF benefits page. That page renders only in a HEADED browser (empty to curl and headless Playwright); last read 2026-10-07. Re-read it the same way each cycle.
- [x] **Grant amounts**, two published tables disagree: IDF page (undated, read 2026-10-07) 601.2 / 467.2 / 233.7 NIS; NBN table as of February 2024 637.11 / 495.05 / 247.68 NIS. The skill quotes both with dates and presents neither as the 2026 figure. Re-read both each cycle (IDF page needs a headed browser).
- [x] **Aliyah-Ministry monthly grant**, olim bodedim (aliyah within 5y before service) get a SECOND grant = 100% of turai base salary (150% for Ethiopian olim within 10y), stacks on the IDF grant; apply via Misrad HaAliyah (03-9733333)
- [x] **Rent + utilities assistance during service**, via Mashak Tash, covers rent plus apartment-maintenance costs (water, gas, vaad bayit). The arnona exemption and the electricity-bill discount are SEPARATE benefits on the same Kol Zchut page, not part of this participation.
- [x] **Free flights home ("מתגעגעים הביתה")**, after 8 months of service: 1 funded flight for non-combat, 2 for combat (not the same year); the funding covers ticket + port fees + medical insurance (soldier pays only passport + entry visas), coordinated via Mashak Tash (kolzchut מימון טיסה)
- [x] **Other often-missed benefits**, psychometric-course funding, private-work permit, subsidized אל"ח / "יחד למען החייל" apartments (higher food-voucher allotment), post-discharge boded scholarships (קרן הישג, קרן גרוס), כנפיים program
- [x] **Housing options during service**, rented apartment (participation + 3-month advance cap + olim addition), arnona exemption, electricity discount (50% of first 400 kWh, contract must be in the soldier's name, call 103), kibbutz (150 NIS/month grant), Alach apartments, Beit HaChayal (permanent or on leave)
- [x] **Leave**, per Pkudat Matkal 35.0808 (public PDF, Feb 2021) §11: errands day at least every two months (monthly in initial training, never Friday or holiday eve), 2 days per move, parent-visit-in-Israel 8 special-leave days per calendar year (4 in initial training or a course), 30-day abroad leave per service year in addition to the special-leave quota (Kol Zchut instead counts it within 60; both stated). Abroad leave is NOT insured by the IDF.
- [x] **Marriage grant**, about 1,080 NIS one-time special grant plus three months of continued boded benefits; discharge after those three months forfeits discharged-lone-soldier benefits (IDF page)
- [x] **Post-discharge 12-month rent assistance**, up to 1,000 NIS/month × 12 months = 12,000 NIS cap; rent only (no bills); two payments of up to 6 months; lease months during service not reimbursed; completion request for the remainder via *5266; new lease within 3 months of the previous one ending; register within the first year; hotline *5266
- [x] **Post-discharge Beit HaChayal**, up to 3 months free after discharge (hachvana עזרה בדיור); months spent there are deducted from the rent period (Kol Zchut לינה בבית החייל)
- [x] **Flight exclusion conflict**, the IDF page excludes children of shlichim ("למעט בן שליחים") while Kol Zchut includes shlichut parents; the skill states both and routes to Mashak Tash
- [x] **Pikadon, same per-tier amounts as regular discharged soldier**, explicit debunk of "Pikadon Mugdal" misconception
- [x] **Extended 10-year window applies to the §7 / §7א benefits ONLY** (§7: bagrut completion or improvement, dedicated mechina tracks, handasaim, vocational training; §7א: first-year bachelor's scholarship, per hachvana's law page). It does NOT extend the Pikadon: 6 purposes for 5 years, then the balance is transferred automatically to the bank account; usable only in Israel.
- [x] **Aliyah intersections only**, Aliyah-Ministry grant, olim rent addition, Pikadon usable only in Israel; citizenship and the absorption basket are routed out (see Out of scope)
- [x] **Diaspora orgs (4 distinct)**, Nefesh B'Nefesh + FIDF (joint LSP), FIDF standalone (housing), Garin Tzabar (pre-aliyah kibbutz prep), Lone Soldier Center in Memory of Michael Levin, Mahal (foreign volunteers)
- [x] **Required documents checklist**, per phase: recognition, active-duty rent, flights, post-discharge rent, Pikadon

## Should cover (advanced / edge cases)

- [x] **Food vouchers**, 150 NIS/month in the Hoger app, 60-day redemption per month; 400 NIS total instead for 'יחד למען החייל' residents; holiday vouchers about 250 NIS per holiday per Kol Zchut, IDF page says the amount varies (IDF benefits page, read 2026-10-07)
- [x] **Status change during service**, report within 14 days (order §8ד); recognition can be granted retroactively when the delay is not the soldier's fault (§9); shlichut-based recognition ends with the shlichut (§8ב)
- [x] **Marriage during service**, about 1,080 NIS grant plus three months of continued boded benefits; discharge after those three months forfeits discharged-lone-soldier benefits (IDF benefits page)
- [x] **Keva**, NOT a lone soldier while in keva or in mandatory service under keva terms (Kol Zchut חייל בודד). An earlier version of this checklist said recognition continues; that was wrong.
- [x] **Pre-enlistment recognition**, raised at the Lishkat HaGiyus interview, Form 7304 (covers mechina / yeshiva candidates as well; no separate rule found)
- [x] **Last months before discharge**, use service-only flight and leave, confirm status recorded, Beit HaChayal vs rent trade-off, first-year registration, hachvana counselling and Telegram channel
- [x] **Health extras**, the hub's glasses and hearing-aid programs are listed by name in additional-benefits.md. The earlier unsourced 'Aguda dental' item was removed.

- [x] **No-contact (chaser oref) recognition**, social worker or school counselor report, financial documents (Kol Zchut)

## Out of scope (explicit, with rationale)

- Tax credit points for olim and discharged soldiers, and how they interact with the boded grant: tax questions route to the tax skills and `israeli-discharged-soldier-navigator` (re-litigated 2026-10-07: users may ask, but no source ties a tax rule to lone-soldier status)
- Mahal volunteers' recognition and post-discharge rights: no verifiable source found (2026-10-07); the skill routes them to the Rakezet Tash and *5266
