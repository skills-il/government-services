## Problem

A recognized lone soldier (chayal boded) gets a supplemental package on top of regular service pay: a monthly grant, rent and utility help during service, funded flights home, extra leave, and up to ₪12,000 of rent assistance in the first year after discharge. The rules are split across IDF Order 35.0808, the IDF and hachvana.mod.gov.il benefit pages, Kol Zchut and the diaspora organizations. People often miss that recognition gates the package, miss first-year deadlines, or let service-only benefits lapse at discharge. This skill maps recognition, the active-duty layer, the post-discharge layer (distinct from the regular discharged-soldier package) and NGO routing.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Who Qualifies as a Lone Soldier (Chayal Boded)

> Source quote (kolzchut): "חייל בודד הוא חייל בשירות חובה שאין לו בארץ תמיכה הורית במהלך שירותו הצבאי"

Three recognized categories (Kol Zchut "חייל בודד"):

| Category | Definition |
|---|---|
| Boded muvhak (מובהק) | Parents live permanently abroad and the soldier made aliyah alone; OR parents emigrated; OR parents are on shlichut abroad for half a year or more |
| Chaser oref mishpachti (חסר עורף משפחתי) | No contact with parents; OR in contact but with no possibility of living with them and they are not a functioning, supporting family unit (Kol Zchut's example: a soldier who left the Haredi community). A soldier who lived with a foster family until 18 may in some cases be recognized (never past age 21) |
| Yatom (יתום) | Orphaned of both parents |

**A soldier in keva, or in mandatory service under keva terms, is not considered a lone soldier** (Kol Zchut: "חייל בשירות קבע או חייל בשירות חובה בתנאי קבע לא ייחשב לחייל בודד"). Someone recognized by Bituach Leumi as a child as entitled to the income-support benefit for an orphaned or abandoned child goes straight to the 24-hour lone-soldier line, 03-7375200.

The governing IDF order is **Pkudat Matkal 35.0808** ("חיילים בודדים, עקרונות, זכויות ונהלים, שירות חובה"), public as a PDF updated February 2021 (https://www.idf.il/media/rqyjrko0/פמ-350808-מעודכנת-פברואר-2021.pdf). It applies to an unmarried soldier in mandatory service who is not entitled to family payments. Kol Zchut also links a multilingual lone-soldier rights page on the IDF site.

### Step 2: Recognition Process

> Source quote (kolzchut): "יש להגיש בקשה ללשכת הגיוס האזורית (למי שטרם התגייס), או לרכזת תנאי השירות (משקית ת\"ש) ביחידה שבה משרתים"

**Two different forms, one per phase, do not swap them** (Kol Zchut "הגשת בקשה להכרה כחייל בודד"). Form 7304 is not internal-only; a pre-enlistment candidate should expect to fill it.

| Phase | What to do | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-enlistment (Lishkat HaGiyus interview) | Raise lone-soldier status at the initial interview, then fill **Form 7304** at the Rakezet Tash's initiative. She forwards it to the office HQ for approval, exceptional cases go to the recognition committee at Maktzar. Approval is mailed home, and the boded certificate is issued on enlistment day. At basic-training intake the Rakezet Tash must brief the soldier personally on their rights. | Regional recruitment office |
| Already in service | Fill **Form 62** (שאלון הכרה לחייל בודד) in two copies | Before an officer or the Rakezet Tash in the unit |

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Documents | Per Kol Zchut: **none needed** for a soldier who made aliyah alone, whose parents emigrated, or whose parents died; parents on shlichut = a letter from the sending body with the shlichut dates, especially the return date; no contact with parents = a report by a social worker or school counselor, financial documents, and whatever else the Rakezet Tash asks for |
| Decision | Recruitment-office HQ, or the Maktzar committee in exceptional cases. No processing time is published; ask the Rakezet Tash. |
| If rejected | Appeal through the Rakezet Tash. The order has two levels: a muvhak refusal goes to the exceptions committee, whose decision can be appealed to a committee headed by the head of the Mofat department (§6-7); each gives reasoned written decisions |
| Hotlines | Kol Zchut: IDF service line **1111**, Soldiers' Ombudsman (נציב קבילות החיילים) **03-6977374**, 24-hour lone-soldier line **03-7375200**. NBN lists the IDF lone-soldier department's line from abroad, **+972-3-5691000**. FIDF and NBN run a 24-hour call center |
| Changes | Report any change in the circumstances behind the recognition to the Tash staff **within 14 days** (order §8ד). Recognition of a muvhak soldier whose parents are on shlichut ends with the shlichut or with service, whichever is earlier (§8ב). Recognition can be granted **retroactively** where a delay in filing or handling was not the soldier's fault or is justified (§9) |

**Recognition gates the lone-soldier package.** None of the grants, rent help, flights or post-discharge rent below applies without it. (Kol Zchut: some lodging can also go to unrecognized soldiers under "הלנה מטעמי פרט".)

### Step 3: Active-Duty Monthly Grant

> Source quote (kolzchut מענק חודשי): "חיילים בודדים זכאים למענק חודשי קבוע, ששיעורו 100% משכר היסוד של טוראי בשירות חובה."

A **fixed monthly grant equal to 100% of a private's (turai) base salary in mandatory service**, paid automatically with the regular pay once recognized, whatever the soldier's rank or tier. Combat and combat-support lone soldiers get an **additional** boded grant on top of it, by activity level (א'+ and א').

**Amounts: two published tables disagree, and neither is dated 2026.**

| Item | IDF benefits page (undated, read 2026-10-07) | NBN lone-soldier benefits table ("as of February 2024") |
|---|---|---|
| Lone-soldier monthly grant | ₪601.2 | ₪637.11 |
| Extra grant, activity level א'+ / combat (NBN calls it "Risk Pay") | ₪467.2 | ₪495.05 |
| Extra grant, activity level א' / combat support (NBN: "Risk Pay") | ₪233.7 | ₪247.68 |

The NBN figures are uniformly higher, so the IDF page is probably older. Give the mechanism, quote both with their dates, and send the user to Mashak Tash for the current amount. Do not present either as the 2026 figure.

**Oleh lone soldiers get a SECOND monthly grant from the Aliyah Ministry that STACKS on the IDF grant.** Per Kol Zchut, it is **100% of a private's base salary** (**150%** for olim from Ethiopia). Eligible: on the enlistment day no more than 5 years have passed since aliyah (10 for olim from Ethiopia), AND the soldier either holds an independent Teudat Oleh with parents not in Israel, or is an oleh, returning minor, ezrach oleh or toshav chozer recognized by the IDF as a lone soldier. Apply at an Aliyah Ministry service bureau (call 03-9733333 first) with Teudat Oleh, a bank confirmation, the lone-soldier certificate and a service confirmation with the expected discharge date, re-presented every half year. **Eligibility is renewed every year** and runs to the end of service. **Past the 5 years is not automatically the end:** the Aliyah Ministry appeals committee handles exceptions, and a soldier more than 3 years in Israel who studied before enlisting may apply as an exception with a Student Authority confirmation. Ethiopian-origin soldiers also have an Amharic IDF help line, 03-5690822. Estranged-track Israeli lone soldiers who are not olim do NOT get this grant.

### Step 4: Housing During Service

> Source quote (kolzchut סיוע בהוצאות דיור): "חיילים בודדים זכאים לקבל מצה\"ל השתתפות בדמי שכירות והוצאות עבור אחזקת הדירה כגון: מים, גז וועד בית."

| Option | Detail |
|---|---|
| Rented apartment | IDF participation in rent plus apartment maintenance (water, gas, vaad bayit), requested via the unit Tash staff with the lease attached. If the lease requires advance payment, up to **3 months** can be paid in advance, so Kol Zchut advises not to commit to paying the whole lease up front, and to add a clause letting the soldier cancel if, for example, the parents make aliyah. An oleh lone soldier who is also entitled to olim rent assistance gets an addition to it. NBN's February 2024 table lists ₪1,482.49 rent assistance and ₪372.39 apartment maintenance a month; treat both as dated |
| Arnona | Exemption, a separate benefit on the same Kol Zchut page |
| Electricity | 50% off the first 400 kWh a month at the domestic rate (Kol Zchut: about ₪129 a month at most, as of January 2026), only for a lone soldier receiving the rent participation. Automatic: the IDF sends a file to the electricity company, matched on the name and ID number of the account holder. **If the contract is in someone else's name the discount will not arrive; transfer the contract.** Missing discount: call 103 |
| Kibbutz | Especially for closed units: furnished room, laundry and food, an "adoptive family", a separate room for combat soldiers, and a **₪150 a month kibbutz grant** |
| Alach / "יחד למען החייל" apartment | IDF-rented, furnished, usually 4 rooms for 6 soldiers, no rent or municipal taxes; vacate on discharge |
| Beit HaChayal | Permanent residence possible, plus free lodging on regular leave, long weekends and holidays at any branch with a free bed (call ahead; show the lone-soldier certificate, military ID and proof of the leave type) |

Active-duty help ends on discharge day; the post-discharge benefit (Step 6) starts then.

### Step 5: Leave, Flights and Other Active-Duty Benefits

| Benefit | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Funded flight home, "מתגעגעים הביתה" | **Only for a lone soldier "מובהק"**; an estrangement-track soldier with family in Israel does not reach it. **The sources disagree on children of shlichim:** Kol Zchut includes parents on shlichut of at least six months, while the IDF benefits page says "כל חייל בודד מובהק, למעט בן שליחים" (every muvhak lone soldier EXCEPT a child of shlichim). Tell a shlichut family the IDF page excludes them and have them confirm with Mashak Tash before buying anything. After **8 months** of service; non-combat **1** funded flight during service, combat **2** (not in the same year), per Kol Zchut's eligibility section, although the same page's summary says funding is given once; confirm the second flight with Mashak Tash. Covers the ticket, port fees AND medical insurance; the soldier pays passport and visas. Passport valid at least six months from the flight date (IDF page); leave approval before booking (Kol Zchut). Request via Mashak Tash. | IDF page + kolzchut |
| Leave to visit parents abroad | Order §11ז: **30 days per service year** (365 days from enlistment) to visit a first-degree relative abroad, **in addition to** the regular special-leave quota, splittable, not carried over. Kol Zchut instead counts these 30 days inside its 60-day special-leave figure; confirm with the Rakezet Tash. The order grants it to any lone soldier with a first-degree relative abroad; Kol Zchut limits it to a muvhak soldier visiting parents. Not to countries barred for field-security reasons. **The IDF does not insure a soldier abroad on leave**; outside the funded flight, buy private travel insurance. Keep a valid Israeli passport for urgent travel; every mandatory soldier gets a passport-fee discount. | order + kolzchut |
| Leave when parents visit Israel | Order §11ו: **8 special-leave days per calendar year** while the parents are visiting, **4** for a soldier in initial training or a course, more if the commander approves; not carried over. (Kol Zchut phrases it as at least 4 per half year and 8 a year.) | order + kolzchut |
| Special leave | For an exceptional personal problem (such as a severe, urgent financial one). Up to **60 days per service year**, of which **30 are only for visiting parents abroad** (kolzchut: "לא יעלו על 60 יום בשנת שירות, כאשר 30 מתוכם... ביקור הורים בחו\"ל"). The first 5 days of each leave come from the regular leave bank. | kolzchut |
| Errands day (יום סידורים) | Order §11ג: at least **once every two months**, coordinated with the commander, not counted against annual or special leave; **once a month in initial training**; never on a Friday or holiday eve. Order §11ד: **2 leave days for each move** of apartment or housing solution. | order + kolzchut |
| Marriage grant | Per the IDF page: a one-time grant of **about ₪1,080**, plus **three months of "התארגנות"** during which boded benefits continue. **A soldier discharged DURING those three months keeps the discharged-lone-soldier benefits (including the Step 6 rent); one discharged AFTER them does not** ("לא יהיה זכאי להטבות"). | IDF page |
| Food and holiday vouchers | IDF page: automatic **₪150** a month in the Hoger app (Kovoret, Shufersal, Yesh Chesed, Yeinot Beitan, Mega BaIr), each month usable for **60 days**; residents of "יחד למען החייל" apartments get **₪400 a month in total instead**. Holiday vouchers for Rosh Hashanah and Pesach: Kol Zchut says about **₪250 per holiday**, the IDF page says the amount changes yearly. | IDF page + kolzchut |

### Step 6: Post-Discharge, 12-Month Rent Assistance via hachvana

> Source quote (hachvana SingleSolders/Rent): "סיוע בשכר דירה בסכום של עד 12,000 ₪ בשנה הראשונה לשחרורכם משירות חובה, לפי הפירוט הבא: עד 1,000 ₪ לחודש, לתקופה של עד 12 חודשי שכירות."

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Amount | Up to 1,000 NIS per month; **below 1,000, the refund is the rent actually paid** |
| Cap | Up to 12 rental months, 12,000 NIS |
| Rent only | Bills are NOT covered: no arnona, electricity, gas or vaad bayit |
| Window | Registration AND the declaration must be filed **within the first year** after mandatory service ends. In the personal area the benefit is labelled by year ("שנת 2026") |
| Document | Since **01.07.2026**: only a **declaration form (טופס הצהרה)** signed by the soldier and the landlord. Check bank details in the personal area first (SMS confirmation within 48 hours) |
| Payments | Two payments of up to 6,000 NIS, each for at most 6 rental months. **The first covers the first 6 months of the lease**; request the second **no later than 4 months** after that period ends, choosing the year of the FIRST request even if the lease runs into the next year. If the first request was filed only after those 6 months had passed, file the second one immediately via `*5266` |
| Lease started during service | Only months after discharge are reimbursed (hachvana's example: lease from 1 January, discharge 1 March, refund for 10 months) |
| Completion to 12 months | If the lease started during service, or a shorter lease was declared, the remainder is still owed: right after the second payment, call `*5266` for a completion request (בקשת השלמה) |
| Moving apartment | Upload a new signed declaration; the new lease must start within **3 months** of the previous one ending. Months already reimbursed count against the 12 |
| Beit HaChayal after discharge | Up to **3 months** free (requested month by month via the hachvana personal area). **Those months are deducted from the rent-assistance period** (Kol Zchut: "יופחתו חודשים אלה מתקופת הזכאות לסיוע בשכר דירה") |
| Timing | Declaration checked within **7 business days** (excluding Fridays and holidays); after SMS approval, payment within **14 business days** |
| Terms may change | By decision of the Fund for the Absorption of Discharged Soldiers. Re-check the page |
| Hotline | hachvana `*5266`; 24-hour lone-soldier line `03-7375200` |

What hachvana does not say: how a month split by a mid-month discharge is prorated, and how lease months past the first anniversary are treated. Do not answer either; send the user to `*5266`.

### Step 6.5: The Last Months Before Discharge

A soldier who is about to be discharged should, in order:

- Use what exists only during service: the funded flight is defined per service period, and leave days belong to service. No source offers either after discharge.
- Confirm with the Rakezet Tash that boded status is recorded through discharge; it is the condition for every post-discharge benefit.
- If marrying, mind the three-month window in Step 5.
- Decide between up to 3 free months in a Beit HaChayal and renting at once, knowing the months come off the rent period.
- Keep the salary bank account open: per hachvana, a discharged lone soldier gets an **advance on the discharge grant within 14 days** (missing: IDF line 1111, extension 2), and the full grant arrives within 60 days to the same account.
- Register for the rent benefit and upload the declaration within the first year; update bank details first.
- Use hachvana's lone-soldier services: personal counselling and a dedicated Telegram channel for discharged lone soldiers.

### Step 7: Lone-Soldier Pikadon + Extended 10-Year Window

There is no lone-soldier "Pikadon Mugdal": the Pikadon follows the same per-tier amounts and the same 6 permitted purposes as for everyone (amounts in `israeli-discharged-soldier-navigator`).

**Easy to get backwards: the 10-year lone-soldier window covers the §7 / §7א BENEFITS, not the Pikadon.** The Pikadon may be used for the 6 purposes during the first 5 years; after that, per hachvana's deposit page, the unused balance is **transferred automatically** to the bank account in the personal area, for any use. Pikadon money can be used **only in Israel** ("ניתנים למימוש בתחומי מדינת ישראל בלבד"). The law page also records that the realization period was extended from 7 to 10 years for everyone discharged after 12.7.2007, a universal rule, not a boded benefit.

| Item | Regular discharged soldier | Lone soldier |
|---|---|---|
| Pikadon for the 6 purposes | First 5 years | First 5 years (**the same**) |
| §7 benefits: completing or improving bagrut, dedicated pre-academic mechina tracks, handasaim, vocational training | 5 years | **10 years** |
| §7א benefit: scholarship for the first year of a bachelor's degree | 5 years | **10 years** |

> Source quote (hachvana, MoD): "חיילים בודדים משוחררים ומשרתי מילואים פעילים יהיו זכאים לקבלת ההטבות בסעיף 7 לחוק ו-7א לחוק כמפורט בסעיף 1, לתקופה של 10 שנים"

> Source: hachvana.mod.gov.il/odot/Pages/hakdamalachock.aspx

A concrete example on hachvana: a discharged lone soldier, within 10 years of discharge at registration, gets **full mechina tuition plus a monthly living stipend** for a dedicated pre-academic or bagrut-completion mechina, in addition to the Pikadon; those discharged July 2021 or later register directly with the mechina using the lone-soldier confirmation from the "הארנק הצה\"לי" app. Hachvana also refunds a psychometric course and test for discharged lone soldiers.

The six Pikadon purposes (studies, vocational training, marriage, a business, driving lessons, buying an apartment or land, NOT rent) and the full mechanics belong to `israeli-discharged-soldier-navigator`.

### Step 8.5: Operational Items and Reserve Duty

Bank details decide whether money arrives: hachvana pays the rent refund and the post-5-year Pikadon balance to the account in the personal area, and the Aliyah-Ministry grant asks for a bank confirmation. Active-duty arnona and electricity relief are not stated to continue after discharge, so do not promise it.

**Reserve duty:** per hachvana, a soldier recognized as boded through discharge is treated as a lone soldier in reserves for **10 years from discharge**; under the Iron Swords definition the reservist must also have done **more than 60 cumulative reserve days** in the war. Route reserve questions to `israeli-miluim-manager` and hachvana's page for lone soldiers in reserves.

Details: `references/operational-items.md`.

## Examples

### Example 1: Oleh chayalet bodedet discharging in two months, parents in the US

User says: "I'm a chayalet bodedet, discharging in two months, parents in the US. What do I get after, and what should I do now?"

Actions:
1. Before discharge (Step 6.5): if she has not used her funded flight or her yearly abroad leave, they exist only during service; confirm her boded status is on record through discharge.
2. After discharge: rent assistance up to 1,000 NIS a month for up to 12 rental months (12,000 NIS), registered and documented within year 1 by a declaration signed with the landlord. Rent only, no bills.
3. If she first takes the up-to-3 free months in a Beit HaChayal, those months come off the rent period.
4. If her lease started before discharge or is shorter than 12 months, she requests completion of the remainder via `*5266` after the second payment.
5. Her §7 / §7א study benefits run 10 years, including a fully funded mechina with a living stipend. Route the regular stack (Pikadon by tier, discharge grant) to `israeli-discharged-soldier-navigator`.

### Example 2: Israeli parent estranged from soldier child

User says: "My daughter is formally recognized as bodedet (we have not been in contact for 3 years). What happens when she finishes the army?"

Actions:
1. Until discharge: monthly grant, housing help, errands days and special leave. The funded flight does not apply (muvhak only). Abroad leave: only with a first-degree relative abroad (Step 5).
2. After discharge: the same post-discharge rent assistance (Step 6) applies whatever the recognition track.
3. Regular discharged-soldier stack: route to `israeli-discharged-soldier-navigator`.
4. She can also contact the Lone Soldier Center and the other organizations in Step 8.

### Example 3: Parent abroad planning a visit, son is a lone soldier

User says: "My son is a lone soldier in the IDF. I'm flying to Israel next month. Can he get time off, and can he fly home later?"

Actions:
1. While a parent visits Israel, a lone soldier gets 8 special-leave days per calendar year (4 in initial training or a course), more with the commander's approval; he asks the Rakezet Tash.
2. Flight home: after 8 months of service, 1 funded flight (2 for combat, not in the same year), ticket, port fees and medical insurance covered. If the family is abroad on shlichut, warn that the IDF page excludes children of shlichim and confirm with Mashak Tash first.
3. The yearly 30-day abroad leave without the funded flight is not insured by the IDF; buy travel insurance.

## Gotchas

- **Apply the moment you are eligible.** Recognition itself can be granted retroactively (order §9), but neither Kol Zchut nor hachvana says whether the active-duty rent participation or the Aliyah-Ministry grant is paid retroactively. Do not answer either way; say every month of delay may be unrecoverable. The phone card is explicitly not refunded retroactively.
- **The flight cap caps ONE program, not all travel abroad.** A soldier who has used the funded flights and then faces a parent's death or critical illness abroad goes straight to Mashak Tash and the Aguda; do not say nothing remains. Kol Zchut also notes that in special cases, at the IDF's request, the Aguda flies family members to Israel.
- **Do not quote a grant amount as current.** The two published tables (Step 3) differ and neither is dated 2026.
- **The post-discharge rent benefit is "first year".** Registration and the declaration must be in within 12 months of discharge; there is no published late route. Set an explicit reminder for olim returning to family abroad temporarily.
- **The monthly grant is 100% of a PRIVATE'S base salary, NOT of the soldier's actual pay.** A combat lone soldier earns the combat rate plus the turai-scale grant.
- **Estrangement-track lone soldiers get the same grant, housing help and post-discharge rent, but not the funded flight or the Aliyah-Ministry grant**, which need muvhak or olim status.
- **Keva ends lone-soldier status.** A soldier in keva, or in mandatory service under keva terms, is not a lone soldier (Kol Zchut).
- **Mahal is NOT the oleh lone-soldier track.** The volunteers' guide site describes Mahal as a gate for non-Israelis and overseas Israelis. Do not assume their benefits either way (Step 8).
- **For the regular discharged-soldier package, route to israeli-discharged-soldier-navigator.** Pikadon by tier, discharge grant, nekudot zikui Section 39a, Mimadim. This skill adds only the lone-soldier layer.

## Troubleshooting

### Error: "Post-discharge rent application rejected, 'no recognition record'"
Cause: The soldier was not recorded as recognized during service. Living alone does not qualify; recognition through Form 7304 or Form 62 is required.
Solution: If recognition WAS granted but is missing from the record, ask the unit Tash staff or the 24-hour lone-soldier line for documentation. If it was never requested, the post-discharge boded benefits cannot be claimed.

### Error: "Discharged 14 months ago, missed the rent application"
Cause: The first-year window has closed.
Solution: hachvana requires registration and the declaration within the first year and publishes no late route, so treat the benefit as lost unless `*5266` says otherwise. Turn to what remains: the §7 / §7א benefits (10 years), the regular stack in `israeli-discharged-soldier-navigator`, and NGO help.

### Error: "Active-duty rent benefit denied because parents recently moved to Israel"
Cause: Eligibility depends on the parental situation; if the parents moved to Israel, the soldier may no longer be a lone soldier.
Solution: If the move is temporary or the soldier still has no real family support, re-present the case to the Rakezet Tash. No source publishes from which date benefits stop, so do not quote one. Kol Zchut advises a lease clause allowing cancellation if the parents make aliyah.

### Error: "Trying to log in to the hachvana personal area, the method I was told to use is not accepted"
Cause: The benefit page does not document the sign-in method, so instructions from other sources may be out of date.
Solution: Use whichever method the hachvana sign-in screen currently offers. If it still fails, call `*5266`.

### Error: "Recognition request rejected despite living alone in Israel"
Cause: Living alone is not one of the recognized categories by itself.
Solution: Appeal through the Rakezet Tash (two levels, Step 2). For a no-contact case, add the social worker or school counselor report and financial documents. Kol Zchut requires no documents from an oleh who came alone, so if such a soldier was rejected, ask the Rakezet Tash exactly what was missing.
