# Domain checklist

This skill is a claim guide. It states no grant amount, rate or formula, and decides no eligibility. The Tax Authority simulator is the only source of an amount.

Must cover: track questions, filing windows with source dates, the red-track check, the base-period concepts, documents, objection and appeal routes, and the already-filed steps.

Sources: the 2026 economic-assistance law, Tax Authority Directive 07/2026, and the official claim pages. Recheck the windows on every update.
